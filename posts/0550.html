You Belong Here

by M. H. Clark · illustrated by Isabelle Arsenault

ISBN 9781938298998 · Find on Bookshop.org

If you’re troubled by life’s hardships, missing an absent loved one, or anxious about an uncertain future, I recommend sitting and falling under the spell of the gorgeous You Belong Here by M. H. Clark and @isabelle_arsenault. On each page, Clark unfolds stanza after stanza of lyrical verse in common meter (alternating lines of four and three metrical feet), likening the tenderness of human connection to animals at home with each other in biomes around the world.
