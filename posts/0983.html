Child of the Universe

by Ray Jayawardhana · illustrated by Raúl Colón

ISBN 9781524717544 · Buy on Bookshop.org

If you’re after a breathtaking fusion of the scientific and the poetic, look no further than Ray Jayawardhana and @raul.colon.7140’s Child of the Universe. At bedtime, a young girl’s father lulls her to sleep with the connections between her own body and the materials of earth and space. In loosely rhyming couplets, Jayawardhana links microcosm and macrocosm—hair swirling like the Milky Way, eyes reflecting faraway planets, atoms spinning like Saturn’s rings.
