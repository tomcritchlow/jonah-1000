The Red Cloak

by Betsy James

ISBN 9780877015642 · Buy on Bookshop.org

Tam Lin Week gallops ahead, leading us to the rollicking delights of The Red Cloak by Betsy James. Here Jan and Tam are the best of friends, raised together since birth in a village by a lake. One fateful day, Tam is spirited away by elves. A whole year passes, and Jan goes to the wisest old woman in town, who gives her a magical red cloak. On Midsummer Eve, the brave girl stages a daring rescue, pulling Tam off his horse and holding him tightly while he turns into a lump of ice, a blazing flame, and many other treacherous shapes. Finally, the elves vanish, leaving the two friends in peace.
