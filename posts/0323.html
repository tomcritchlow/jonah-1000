There’s a haunting verbal and visual beauty to Jinnie Ghost by Berlie Doherty and @janerayillustration. An ethereal phantom girl flits through the darkness, entering house after house and uses the thoughts, fears, and dreams of sleeping children as raw material to shape their nights into playful flights of fancy. Doherty crafts her prose deliberately, with enchanting refrains that seem to call out to Jinnie Ghost, invoking her as a benevolent but inscrutable spirit.
