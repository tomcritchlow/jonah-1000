The Magic Pumpkin

by Bill Martin Jr. and John Archambault · illustrated by Robert J. Lee

ISBN 9780805049046 · Find on Bookshop.org

Halloween inches closer, and this week of seven pumpkin books continues with The Magic Pumpkin, written by Bill Martin Jr. and John Archambault and illustrated by Robert J. Lee. The narrator heads out into the garden to choose a pumpkin to carve into a jack-o’-lantern. One pumpkin whispers that it will keep foolies out of the yard. Intrigued, the narrator selects it and gives it eyes, a nose, and a jagged grin. However, a wild little band of foolies soon tramples into the yard, and the pumpkin is revealed to be the leader of their puckish gang. Vexed, the narrator snuffs out the candle inside the treacherous gourd, which quickly withers away.
