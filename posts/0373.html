The Great Kettles: A Tale of Time

by Dean Morrissey

ISBN 9780810933965 · Find on Bookshop.org

If you’d like a luminous cosmic adventure, I submit for your perusal Dean Morrissey’s The Great Kettles: A Tale of Time. This sequel to Morrissey’s earlier title Ship of Dreams continues the story of young Joey, who discovers the remnants of a time machine in his new house and crosses a mysterious barrier into a realm of metaphysical marvels. Guided by Father Time himself, he sets out across the Great Kettles, an extraordinary archipelago where dwell the keepers of Earth, Sun, Moon, and Stars, in an effort to return to the moment of his departure from home.
