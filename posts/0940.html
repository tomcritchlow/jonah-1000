They All Saw a Cat

by Brendan Wenzel

ISBN 9784062830980 · Find on Bookshop.org

Is there any such thing as objectivity? That is, when you look at a thing and I look at that thing, do we see the same thing? @brendan_wenzel’s They All Saw a Cat takes a marvelous deep dive into these deceptively deep questions. The story, stripped down to the utmost simplicity, shifts perspectives constantly from page to page. A cat takes a walk, passing by numerous animals as it goes. Each creature perceives the cat differently—the dog sees a miserable, slinking wretch; the mouse sees a hulking, taloned fiend; the fly sees a mosaic of spots; the snake sees a glowing heat signature; the bat sees a splash of echolocated texture. Wenzel pairs his quietly philosophical writing with vividly expressive art executed in a variety of media, including colored pencils, oil pastels, acrylic paint, watercolor, charcoal, marker, and digital technique.
