Castles, Caves, and Honeycombs

by Linda Ashman · illustrated by Lauren Stringer

ISBN 9780439857062 · Find on Bookshop.org

What does your home look like? Does it shelter you and keep you safe? Does it have just the right amount of space for you and the ones you love? Does it reflect your identity? Imagine you are an animal. What might your home look like now? For a gorgeous exploration of the spaces that various members of the animal kingdom call home, I highly recommend Castles, Caves, and Honeycombs by Linda Ashman and @lauren_stringer_artist_author. Ashman employs rhyming couplets in iambic tetrameter, taking the reader through places they may not have thought of as home before: a sand dune, a mound of snow, a rocky cliff, a tidal pool.
