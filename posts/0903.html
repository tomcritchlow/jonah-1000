@guojing.art has crafted a marvelous wordless story, perfectly blending the cosmic and the intimate: The Only Child. Feeling lonely, a young child decides to go on an excursion by bus—without any adults. Stranded in the snow, the child soon encounters a majestic stag, who offers warmth and protection. The two embark on a mystical journey, which takes them up a cloudy stair, into the sky, and inside the belly of a whale, which contains a field of tiny glowing lights. Afterward, it is time for a flight home in the moonlight and a gentle goodbye.
