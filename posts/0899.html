The book of the day is Rob D. Walker and Leo and Diane Dillon’s Mama Says. Each page depicts the loving bond between a mother and a son in a variety of cultures throughout history. The Dillons’ rich-hued art takes readers on a journey through time and space, depicting families engaged in a multitude of activities, including baking, studying, performing, and creating art.
