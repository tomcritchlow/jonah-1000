Little Hobbin

by Theodor Storm · illustrated by Lisbeth Zwerger

ISBN 9781558584617 · Find on Bookshop.org

Take a gander at the fanciful tale of Little Hobbin, an original work by nineteenth-century German author Theodor Storm, illustrated by the renowned Lisbeth Zwerger. In this peculiar bedtime story, a young boy’s boundless energy overwhelms all those who seek to bundle him off to sleep...even the moon itself.
