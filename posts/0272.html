The Balloon Tree

by Phoebe Gilman

ISBN 9781552091517 · Find on Bookshop.org

Princess Leora loves balloons. Her uncle the Archduke hates them. From the conflict inherent in this simple premise, Phoebe Gilman crafts a merry original adventure in The Balloon Tree. When Leora’s father the king is invited to a tournament in a foreign kingdom, he tells her that if trouble arises she need only release a bunch of balloons to signal him. Once he departs, the wicked Archduke immediately stages a coup, usurping the throne and ordering every balloon in the kingdom destroyed. On the advice of her friend the Wizard, Leora must find one last balloon and bury it beneath a tree in the castle courtyard to trigger a magical phenomenon.
