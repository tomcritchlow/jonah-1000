The Snow Country Prince

by Daisaku Ikeda · illustrated by Brian Wildsmith

ISBN 9780517104668 · Find on Bookshop.org

For a loving ode to the inherent kindness of children, come and peruse Geraldine McCaughrean’s adaptation of Japanese author and philosopher Daisaku Ikeda’s The Snow Country Prince, illustrated by @brianwildsmithofficial. Young siblings Mariko and Kazuo, residents of a land called the Snow Country, care for swans struggling to find food during a particularly harsh winter. Their efforts catch the attention of the Snow Country Prince, a mysterious nature spirit and guardian of the birds who commends them on their selfless behavior and watches over them invisibly from then on.
