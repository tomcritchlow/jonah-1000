Sebastian and the Balloon

by Philip C. Stead

ISBN 9789866273643 · Find on Bookshop.org

Here is Sebastian and the Balloon, written and illustrated by Philip C. Stead, one half of @number5bus. If you’re feeling bored, listless, or apathetic, this book just might have a little something for you. Sebastian sits on his roof, looking around and spotting absolutely nothing of interest. He gathers everything he will ever need, piles it into a hot air balloon of his own making, and soars into the sky. On his voyage, he meets and befriends a real bear, a tall bird, and three knitting sisters. The motley crew bands together to fix up an old roller coaster and they spend a delightful day careening around the rickety track.
