The Proverbial Mouse

by Moira Miller · illustrated by Ian Deuchar

ISBN 9780803701953 · Find on Bookshop.org

A gentle amalgam of whimsy and wordplay animates Moira Miller and Ian Deuchar’s The Proverbial Mouse. Miller crafts the lighthearted tale of a mouse hungrily exploring a quiet shop after dark, constantly mistaking assorted playthings for food. Upon each error, a doll or figurine springs to life and admonishes the innocent creature with a well-known proverb, occasionally tweaked or reinterpreted for comic effect.
