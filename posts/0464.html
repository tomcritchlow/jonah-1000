The Night Children

by Sarah Yi-Mei Tsiang · illustrated by Delphine Bodet

ISBN 9781429950176 · Find on Bookshop.org

So often in literature the night bears connotations of the unknown, of threat and ill omen. How refreshing, then, and how enchanting to consider the darkness as a space for mirth, energy, and joyful play. To that end, here is @sarah_yi_mei and @delphinebodet’s The Night Children. Each day after sundown, a mysterious troupe of young revelers comes forth to skip and leap among the gathering shadows, frolicking wildly until the dawn.
