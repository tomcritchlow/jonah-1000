#104 of 1001 · November 12, 2020

The book of the day is Allan Baillie and Wayne Harris’s DragonQuest, an original tale filled with impressive terrain, frightening beasts, and unexpected discoveries. A grizzled knight, in florid and theatrical tones, invites a boy to accompany him on a hunt to face the last dragon atop Glass Mountain. Baillie’s text is entirely restricted to the paladin’s self-aggrandizing speech as he narrates their journey across barren deserts, through haunted forests, and along treacherous cliffs. Harris, with a blend of acrylic painting and digital media, depicts one dangerous obstacle after another, including poisonous vermin, soul-ensnaring witches, demons, werewolves, and a two-headed troll. At the final mountain peak, the warrior laments that no dragon has appeared before them.
