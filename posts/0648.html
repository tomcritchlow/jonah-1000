The Man Who Kept His Heart in a Bucket

by Sonia Levitin · illustrated by Jerry Pinkney

ISBN 9780590459228 · Find on Bookshop.org

In Sonia Levitin and @jerrypinkneystudio’s The Man Who Kept His Heart in a Bucket, magical realism gives way to a sweet story of loneliness, warmth, and friendship. Jack lives in a little village, earning a living by crafting goods out of metal. While he does procure a satisfactory wage, he seems unable to enjoy himself or feel positive emotions, for he keeps his heart in a bucket—in order to protect himself from heartbreak. One day, a fish transforms itself into a mysterious maiden before Jack’s eyes, snatches his heart away, and challenges him to solve a riddle. As Jack puzzles out the answer, he slips closer and closer to forming positive connections with those around him.
