Ten Birds

by Cybèle Young

ISBN 9781554539550 · Find on Bookshop.org

Come follow along with me as I work my way down through the numerical world of Ten Birds by @cybeleyoung. This clever counting-down book opens in a barren landscape with ten odd sculptures standing by the side of a wide river. A flock of blackbirds arrives and wishes to cross to the other side. One by one, each bird approaches a different sculpture, detaches one physical piece, fashions it into a transportation device, and nimbly traverses the river. Young executes her elegantly simple concept book with an appealing economy of language and bold black and white illustrations. In a clever design choice, the birds’ removal of the structural components transforms the sequence of sculptures into numerals in descending order from ten to one.
