There’s a certain magic to that hazy hour when night has fallen and it’s time to return to the warmth and comfort of home. That magic is deftly captured by @idapearle in The Moon Is Going to Addy’s House. It is the early evening. Addy’s mother arrives to pick her up from a playdate at her friend’s house. The moon appears to be following the family as they drive their car out of the city beneath towering buildings, across a long bridge over a wide body of water, past hills and valleys, all the way to their house. After a bath, Addy dances, flips, and twirls outside before going in for bed.
