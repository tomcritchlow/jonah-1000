Beauty and the Beast Week now brings us to this fascinating version by Etienne Delessert, who uses the the tale’s supernatural details as jumping-off points for his bizarre imagination. Humans, animals, and objects appear in his trademark grotesque style. Whimsical details include a large, hanging spider with flashlight eyes, a flock of birds playing dominoes, and a Beast in the shape of a large eagle with paws and a fanged beak. A note for folklore enthusiasts: this edition incorrectly attributes the source material to French writer Marie-Catherine Le Jumel de Barneville, Baroness d’Aulnoy. The true author of the original tale is Gabrielle-Suzanne Barbot de Villeneuve.
