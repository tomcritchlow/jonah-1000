Shrek!

by William Steig

ISBN 9780141374710 · Find on Bookshop.org

Here’s an oddball classic that revels in playful wickedness: Shrek! by William Steig. The title character, born to monstrous parents in a dank hole in the ground, sets out to make his way in the world, crossing paths with a clairvoyant witch, a menacing forest dragon, sentient lightning and thunder, a listless donkey, a pugnacious knight, and a hideous princess. Steig relates his story with an impish amorality. Each encounter drives home the central theme—Shrek is horrible and he loves it.
