Baby Bear

by Kadir Nelson

ISBN 9780062241726 · Find on Bookshop.org

@kadirnelson’s Baby Bear explores poignant feelings of anxiety and isolation, emotions we have surely all experienced at times. The plot begins in medias res, with the title character lost and alone. As Baby Bear traverses the wilderness, each animal along the way offers a unique piece of advice, much of it applicable in life: trust yourself, hug a tree, sing a song, listen to your heart. Nelson eschews narration, propelling the story forward through gentle dialogue alone.
