Beauty and the Beast

by Anne Carter · illustrated by Binette Schroeder

ISBN 9780517561737 · Find on Bookshop.org

A deep transformation is upon us as we continue through our second Beauty and the Beast Week. Have a look at this fascinating collaboration between Anne Carter and Binette Schroeder. Carter’s expansive, opulent text closely follows the French of Madame Jeanne-Marie Leprince de Beaumont. Schroeder’s spare illustrations give off a ghostly atmosphere, flecked with bits of surrealism.
