Humble Pie

by Jennifer Donnelly · illustrated by Stephen Gammell

ISBN 9789570368826 · Buy on Bookshop.org

Humble Pie, written by Jennifer Donnelly and illustrated by Stephen Gammell is a jocular and fanciful look at the depths of one child’s insolence and the lengths to which one family member will go to transform his attitude. Theo’s grows up in a family with loving parents who give him everything his heart desires. However, unappreciative of their doting and fawning, he develops into an obstreperously behaved child. One day, his grandmother chants a magic spell and traps him into an enormous pie. Theo rolls this way and that, but nobody can understand his pleas for assistance. He finally escapes and returns home a contrite and dutiful son, having eaten “humble pie”.
