The Moonrat and the White Turtle

by Helen Ward

ISBN 9781840118735 · Buy on Bookshop.org

I’ve been enjoying myself perusing Helen Ward’s The Moonrat and the White Turtle, the lively and eloquent tale of a band of animal pirates on a quest to capture the moon itself. Ward strikes an appropriate balance between judgment and compassion in her treatment of the chieftain’s hubris and eventual comeuppance.
