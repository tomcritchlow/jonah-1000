Wynken, Blynken, and Nod

by Eugene Field · illustrated by Johanna Westerman

ISBN 9780695897307 · Buy on Bookshop.org

If gentle rhythm and rhyme relaxes you, you’ll surely love Wynken, Blynken, and Nod, a renowned poem by Eugene Field with illustrations by Johanna Westerman. Three children sail up into the night sky, riding in a mystical boat formed from a wooden clog. The nautical vessel is just the thing, as the starry world above appears to them as a wide open sea, with the moon a friendly guide upon the surface of the waters. They cast a net outward and haul in a catch of twinkly herring before returning to their nursery to lay their heads down and sleep.
