An elusive and engaging puzzle is Museum Trip, a wordless adventure by Barbara Lehman. Separated from his school group on a field trip to the museum, a boy notices a secret door, behind which lies a case displaying a collection of drawn mazes. As he peers at one of the images, he suddenly finds himself shrunk to a few inches tall, able to explore the labyrinthine twists and turns on foot. Lehman employs a modern style with clean lines and block colors in her gouache, watercolor, and ink images, shifting to a faded, diagrammatic appearance for the antique maze drawings.
