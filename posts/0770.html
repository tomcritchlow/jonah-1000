The Camel's Lament

by Charles Edward Carryl · illustrated by Charles Santore

ISBN 9780375814266 · Find on Bookshop.org

For a chuckle, check out Charles Santore’s eye-popping illustrations for Charles Edward Carryl’s classic poem The Camel’s Lament. The book presents a series of animals, charmingly depicted and well considered. A camel, narrating the text, compares itself to each other creature, bemoaning its relatively shabby position when it comes to diet, sleep, housing, passengers, and body shape.
