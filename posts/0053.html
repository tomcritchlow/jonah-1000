The Red Tree

by Shaun Tan

ISBN 9780968876831 · Find on Bookshop.org

If you’re searching for a profoundly moving experience, I encourage you to get your hands on a copy of The Red Tree by the endlessly inventive @shauncytan. The second-person text acknowledges a series of universal discomforts and melancholies, accompanied by extraordinary vignettes that probe the bleak corners of a faintly quirky dystopia: A dire warning of nightmarish things to come? The externalization of a lonely human’s inner suffering? Perhaps both. The unnamed protagonist’s indifference to the heightened imagery of the daily routine suggests numbness, a quiet acceptance of the unbalanced and unforgiving world.
