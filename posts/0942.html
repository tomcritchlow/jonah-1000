The People Could Fly

by Virginia Hamilton · illustrated by Leo and Diane Dillon

ISBN 9782732034348 · Find on Bookshop.org

Virginia Hamilton and Leo and Diane Dillon show the power of story to strengthen and uplift in The People Could Fly. This deeply emotional work follows a group of African people captured, stolen, and enslaved. Forced to work in cotton fields, they begin to lose hope in the face of ceaseless violence and abuse. An old man known as Toby sees the suffering of his people and comes to their aid: he whispers words of ancient mysticism into their ears, and so grants them the gift of flight, the ability to escape their oppressors for good.
