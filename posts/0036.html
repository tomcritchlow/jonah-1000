Dove Isabeau

by Jane Yolen · illustrated by Dennis Nolan

ISBN 9780152241315 · Find on Bookshop.org

Here’s Dove Isabeau, a superb tale of enchantment, betrayal, and true understanding written by Jane Yolen and illustrated by Dennis Nolan. Familiar fantasy tropes abound, but Yolen boldly reconfigures and refracts them: maiden as monster, prince as healer. With each watercolor painting, Nolan pulls the reader into a riveting world of sea foam on cliff, of torchlight on stair, of crystal balls, secret runes, and talking cats.
