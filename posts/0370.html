The Night Call

by Jeanne Modesitt · illustrated by Robin Spowart

ISBN 9780670825004 · Find on Bookshop.org

As you sink slowly into sleepiness, have a look at The Night Call by wife-and-husband author-illustrator team Jeanne Modesitt and Robin Spowart. When a star falls from the sky, the mysterious Starman sends out a call, prompting Rabbit and Mr. Bear to journey through a secret door, down a twisting stair, and into a sunlit pastoral world. Together with their friends Giraffe, Donkey, and Pup, they locate the star and give it to Starman for safekeeping.
