The Barnabus Project

by Terry Fan, Eric Fan, and Devin Fan

ISBN 9780735263260 · Find on Bookshop.org

If the goal is perfection, what happens to those who cannot measure up? Flawed beings with bulges, blotches, and blemishes have as much intrinsic worth as anyone else—and this is the thesis of @thefanbrothers’ charming original tale The Barnabus Project. Deep under the city, below a shop that proudly advertises genetically engineered Perfect Pets, lies a secret laboratory where scientists conduct shadowy experiments to produce cuddly and appealing little animals. Failed Projects are stored in bell jars and left on shelves to gather dust. One of these discarded creatures is Barnabus, a half-mouse, half-elephant who dreams of seeing the world outside.
