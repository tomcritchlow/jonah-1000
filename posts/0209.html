Cinderella

by Beni Montresor

ISBN 9780394826196 · Buy on Bookshop.org

Continuing with Cinderella Week, have a look at this fascinating version by Beni Montresor. Rather different from those I’ve posted so far, it draws inspiration from the 1817 opera La Cenerentola, ossia La bontà in trionfo (“Cinderella, or Goodness Triumphant”) by Gioacchino Rossini.
