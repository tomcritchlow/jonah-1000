Day Dreamers

by Emily Winfield Martin

What hovers beyond the boundaries of thought, waiting to make itself known when the mind wanders? Here is a volume that explores that gentle and fascinating premise: Day Dreamers, @emilydreamworld’s companion to Dream Animals. In this ode to imagination and creativity, children relax and are beckoned inward to revel in the company of mythical creatures in marvelous surroundings, including a phoenix in the time of dinosaurs, unicorns in an idyllic forest, and a griffin leading a parade of anthropomorphic animals.
