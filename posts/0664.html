Out of Sight! Out of Mind!

by Claude LaPointe

ISBN 9780152009564 · Buy on Bookshop.org

Out of Sight! Out of Mind! by Claude LaPointe gives readers a wry look at the perils of closing oneself off to nature and the outside world. In this mostly wordless book, a group of children remains indoors to play video games and misses an exciting sequence of events right beneath their own window involving a molehill, a hunter, and a cat and dog engaging in fearsome combat.
