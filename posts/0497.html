The Twelve Dancing Princesses

by Mary Hoffmann · illustrated by Miss Clara

ISBN 9780785723400 · Find on Bookshop.org

Come take my hand and we’ll sweep through this delicate and distinctive Twelve Dancing Princesses retelling by Mary Hoffmann and Miss Clara. Hoffmann applies an air of refinement to her adaptation of the story, with all twelve princesses named after flowers: Violette, Delphine, Iris, Jessamine, Lilou, Véronique, Verveine, Eglantine, Amarante, Marguerite, Rose, and Amandine.
