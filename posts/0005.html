Heckedy Peg

by Audrey Wood · illustrated by Don Wood

ISBN 9780547540214 · Find on Bookshop.org

Now, here is one of my absolute favorite picture books of all time: Heckedy Peg, written and illustrated by @audreywoodauthor and Don Wood. The creative team (and married couple) achieves a true unity of word and image, the spare and elegant language complemented by the haunting beauty of the oil paintings. This story of a mother protecting her seven children from a menacing witch, though it reads like a classic fairy tale, is in fact an original narrative inspired by a sixteenth-century children’s game. The folkloric elements lend the book a striking and timeless quality that transports me straight back to my childhood as I hold it in my hands.
