The Hunting of the Snark: An Agony in Eight Fits

by Lewis Carroll · illustrated by Oleg Lipchenko

ISBN 9781979212410 · Find on Bookshop.org

Have a nibble of high nonsense with this version of Lewis Carroll’s inscrutably odd poem The Hunting of the Snark: An Agony in Eight Fits, illustrated by Oleg Lipchenko. An intrepid Bellman and his plucky crew of alliterative adventurers—a Boots, a Barrister, a Broker, a Billiard-marker, a Banker, a Beaver, a Baker, and a Butcher—set sail in search of the mythical Snark. Just what is a Snark? Nobody knows, perhaps not even Carroll himself. Though the quarry remains elusive, the quest endlessly entertains.
