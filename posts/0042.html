The Winter Wren

by Brock Cole

ISBN 9780374484088 · Find on Bookshop.org

At the intersection of fantasy adventure, human drama, and poetic symbolism lies Brock Cole’s The Winter Wren. The quest of the young hero can be read simultaneously on three levels: the literal (rescuing his sister from a supernatural abduction), the emotional (atoning to his mother for his clumsy waste of food during a famine), and the allegorical (contending with personified manifestations of the passing seasons).
