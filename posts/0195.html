Let’s wander together through the gentle worlds of fairy magic. Come and enjoy a reading of the deeply soothing Good Night, Fairies by wife-and-husband team Kathleen and Michael Hague. At bedtime, a young child begins to wonder about fairies: What do they do? Where do they go? How do they live? The mother dutifully answers every question, verbally building a world of fairy lights, fairy toys, fairy beds, fairy songs, fairy rainbows, fairy dances, and fairy dreams.
