In need of a good shiver? Then check out The Witches and the Singing Mice, a traditional Celtic tale retold by Jenny Nimmo and illustrated by Angela Barrett. When three sinister figures arrive in a little town and afflict the local children with a strange sickness, two plucky cats set out to unravel the mystery and break the fell enchantment.
