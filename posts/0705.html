Rain Makes Applesauce

by Julian Scheer · illustrated by Marvin Bileck

ISBN 9780823400911 · Find on Bookshop.org

You’re sure to furrow your brow and grin with amusement when reading Rain Makes Applesauce by Julian Scheer and Marvin Bileck. This delightful book contains page after page of nonsensical propositions such as, “The stars are made of lemon juice,” “Dolls go dancing on the moon,” and “Monkeys eat the chimney smoke,” with the title occurring as a regular refrain.
