Cinderella

by Amy Ehrlich · illustrated by Susan Jeffers

ISBN 9780241117804 · Buy on Bookshop.org

Here at the halfway point in my weeklong exploration of Cinderella picture books we have a collaboration between author Amy Ehrlich and illustrator Susan Jeffers. Ehrlich works from Charles Perrault’s text, retaining the detail that only the younger stepsister uses the name “Cendrillon” (“Cinderella”); she deals with the other epithet, “Culcendron” (literally “Cinder-Ass”), by applying the common bowdlerized translation “Cinderwench” (preserving the contempt but not the shocking vulgarity of the original taunt). Jeffers employs her preferred cross-hatching technique in fine-line pen, layered with rich inks and dyes, to create scenes of strong narrative detail.
