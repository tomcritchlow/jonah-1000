Nighttime Ninja

by Barbara DaCosta · illustrated by Ed Young

ISBN 9780316230735 · Buy on Bookshop.org

There’s more than meets the eye at first glance in this lightly suspenseful narrative with a playful twist: Barbara DaCosta and Ed Young’s Nighttime Ninja. In the dark, a grappling hook swings up and catches on a roof. A ninja stealthily enters the house and creeps from room to room. Finally he reaches his target, a tub of chocolate ice cream. But as he begins to eat, an adversary appears: his mother. DaCosta cleverly disguises the ninja’s identity as a young boy, presenting his youthful excursion in search of forbidden treats through the lens of his imagination.
