Jack and the Beanstalk

by Anthea Bell · illustrated by Aljoscha Blau

ISBN 9780735813748 · Find on Bookshop.org

Another Jack and the Beanstalk entry of interest is this oddball adaptation by Anthea Bell and Aljoscha Blau. Bell provides a straightforward and respectful adaptation of Joseph Jacobs’ telling, sprinkling a few well-chosen bits of original phrasing throughout the traditional dialogue and story beats. It is in Blau’s illustrations, however, that a unique and engrossing vision arises. Strangeness abounds on every page. Jack rides to the market on a penny-farthing, towing his cow in an oversized basket, only to meet the mysterious bean-giving stranger driving a steampunk jalopy with a miniature boiler for an engine and a tufted armchair seat. Up at the top of the beanstalk, the ogre resembles a lethargic man in pants and an undershirt, while his wife sports the inordinately disturbing combination of an uncanny doll mask over her face and a boning knife hanging from her apron pocket.
