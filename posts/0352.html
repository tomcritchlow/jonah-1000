Beauty and the Beast

by Marianna Mayer · illustrated by Mercer Mayer

ISBN 9781413126648 · Find on Bookshop.org

Let’s dive back into the world of fairy tales with another themed week of exploration—this time around, it’s Beauty and the Beast! To begin, we have a version by former spouses Marianna and Mercer Mayer. Her elegant text and his lush illustrations combine to form a rich and ethereal setting filled with enticing details. The beast’s palace contains subtle but unmistakable imagery drawn from ancient Egyptian art: pharaonic columns, carved hieroglyphs in cartouches, jackals, falcons, serpents, bulls, scarabs, and a large stone sphinx. The beast himself resembles a ferocious, digitigrade otter. A note for folklore enthusiasts: Beauty and the Beast was originally a novella-length story (of about one hundred pages) written by Gabrielle-Suzanne Barbot de Villeneuve in her 1740 collection La Jeune Américaine et Les Contes Marins. In 1756, the tale was substantially abridged by Jeanne-Marie Leprince de Beaumont in Magasin des Enfants.
