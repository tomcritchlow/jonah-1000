The Mirrorstone

by Michael Palin · illustrated by Alan Lee and Richard Seymour

ISBN 9784001106022 · Find on Bookshop.org

Enter the looking glass. Enter a reflected universe of wonder and danger. Enter a work of classic intrigue and suspense. Enter the mixed-media adventure of The Mirrorstone by Michael Palin, Alan Lee, and Richard Seymour. Beckoned through a mirror by his uncooperative reflection, Paul finds himself in an eerie world of medieval magic. At the behest of Salaman, an ageless sorcerer with foul intentions, Paul must undertake a perilous quest to retrieve a lost miracle artifact known the Mirrorstone.
