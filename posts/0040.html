Jack and the Beanstalk

by Steven Kellogg

ISBN 9780613023696 · Find on Bookshop.org

You’re in for a rollicking adventure with Steven Kellogg’s Jack and the Beanstalk. An irresistible vitality emanates from the characters and settings on every page. In adapting the text from Joseph Jacobs’ English Fairy Tales, Kellogg has wisely preserved the storyteller’s voice by retaining many of the source material’s charming turns of phrase in both narration and dialogue.
