Mole Music

by David McPhail

ISBN 9780805028195 · Find on Bookshop.org

There’s a warm and inspiring message buried deep in the core of David McPhail’s Mole Music. The story takes place in a cozy underground burrow. Mole sees a man playing the violin on television and decides to send away for a violin of his own. At first, the notes he produces are all ugly and shrill, but he practices and practices until he can play beautifully. As McPhail unfolds his gentle story, he cleverly juxtaposes Mole’s idle thoughts about playing in front of an audience with the profound effects (unmentioned in the text) his melodies have as they float up above ground.
