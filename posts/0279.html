It’s a true pleasure to read this spellbinding tale of witchcraft and wonder, The Girl in the Golden Bower by Jane Yolen and Jane Dyer. A woodsman, his mysterious wife, and their golden-haired daughter are menaced by a sorceress who seeks a hidden charm to reveal a priceless treasure. The plot twists and turns with long-buried secrets and startling transformations: the imperiled family’s fate lies intertwined with that of a vanished king and an unseen beast lurking in the wood. Only at the denouement are the obscured connections laid bare.
