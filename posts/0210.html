Cinderella

by Barbara McClintock

ISBN 9780439561457 · Find on Bookshop.org

Cinderella Week rolls right along with this retelling by @barbaramcclintockart, who, adapting her material freely from Perrault, translates the crass “Culcendron” as “Cinderbottom” and adds such merry details as the fairy godmother’s oddball incantation, “FOOMUS BALOOMUS!” The illustrations, created with pen, India ink, and watercolor, provide an abundance of rococo ornamentation.
