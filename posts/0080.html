The Boy and the Cloth of Dreams

by Jenny Koralek · illustrated by James Mayhew

ISBN 9781564029751 · Buy on Bookshop.org

If it’s a quietly beautiful story of nighttime fears and the protective power of love you’re after, then look no further: here’s Jenny Koralek and @mrjamesmayhew’s The Boy and the Cloth of Dreams, a tale that pays true respect to the wild and dangerous country we traverse once we close our eyes. A young child, whose sleep has never been anything but pleasant thanks to a special blanket sewn by his grandmother, accidentally rips the cloth. Now plagued by nightmares, he must help his grandmother repair the damage and gain a measure of courage along the way.
