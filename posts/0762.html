The Island Light

by Rosemary Wells

ISBN 9780803711785 · Buy on Bookshop.org

Here is a look at The Island Light, one of three volumes in the Voyage to the Bunny Planet series by Rosemary Wells. Felix spends a miserable day getting sick at school and visiting the doctor. While sitting alone in bed without a goodnight kiss, he is spirited away to the Bunny Planet by the kindly Bunny Queen Janet, who shows him through a mystical arch and grants him the day he should have had. Felix finds himself on a dock in a gentle evening rain with his lighthouse-keeper father, bringing back groceries from the mailboat for a cozy night of pancakes, gingerbread, and card games. At midnight, he wakes up, happy and relaxed. Wells grants her protagonist’s wish in fantasy rather than reality, employing a startling textual shift from third-person prose to poignant rhyming couplets in iambic tetrameter.
