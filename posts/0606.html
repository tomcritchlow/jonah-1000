A Fairy Went A-Marketing

by Rose Fyleman · illustrated by Jamichael Henterly

ISBN 9780613715911 · Find on Bookshop.org

Have a look at Rose Fyleman’s delicate poem A Fairy Went A-Marketing, illustrated by Jamichael Henterly. In each of the four seasons, a gentle fairy makes a purchase from the market (a fish in summer, a bird in fall, a gown in winter, a mouse in spring), keeps it to enjoy for a time, and then gives it to the wilds of nature.
