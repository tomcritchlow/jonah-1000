Imagine a Place

by Sarah L. Thomson · illustrated by Rob Gonsalves

ISBN 9781416968023 · Buy on Bookshop.org

Here is Imagine a Place, a breathtaking combination of the writing of Sarah L. Thomson and the art of Rob Gonsalves. This collection of words and pictures outlines an abstract place that is everywhere and nowhere. Landscapes bridge disparate dimensions, houses contain impossible physical constructs, and people traverse gossamer boundaries. Thomson’s hypnotic phrases (“spring becomes/an avalanche of blossoms”, “space is measured/in sunset”, “fire is cool/against your skin,/a glimmering echo/of a star”) evoke lush and gripping fantasy images.
