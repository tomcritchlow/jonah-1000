As the City Sleeps

by Stephen T. Johnson

Eerie imagery abounds in Stephen T. Johnson’s As the City Sleeps. Each page features a tableau of an urban space—some modern and well-kept, some fallen into disrepair—with a hint, a glimpse, or an unmistakable sighting of the paranormal. Here a group of monkeys climbing out of a picture frame, there an ebullient spider perched on a billboard, then an open manhole cover with a snake slithering in…or is it a tentacle reaching out?
