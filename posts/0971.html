Iron John

by Marianna Mayer · illustrated by Winslow Pels

ISBN 9780688115555 · Find on Bookshop.org

Marianna Mayer and Winslow Pels collaborate on a handsome adaptation of the Brothers Grimm tale Iron John. A young prince named Hans frees a wild man from a cage in his father’s courtyard. Fearing punishment, he flees to the forest. The wild man becomes his guardian and eventually helps him win a fierce battle and a challenging tournament for the hand of a princess. Mayer crafts an elegant text, while Pels employs large-scale compositions that evoke the flattened perspective of medieval art. A note for folklore enthusiasts: Mayer keeps the architecture of the plot contained in the story included by Brothers Grimm in the sixth edition of Kinder- und Hausmärchen (1850). Into that structure she weaves numerous additional details. For example, the young prince is now named Hans (taken from the Grimms’ title “Iron Hans”).
