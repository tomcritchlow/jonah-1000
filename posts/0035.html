When the Sky Is Like Lace

by Elinor Lander Horwitz · illustrated by Barbara Cooney

ISBN 9780670059096 · Find on Bookshop.org

I’ve always been deeply fascinated with When the Sky Is Like Lace by Elinor Lander Horwitz and Barbara Cooney. Much of it written in the second person, the text comprises a set of enigmatic instructions for slipping outside after dark and exploring a moonlit world of small marvels. Horwitz’s language dances playfully through mesmerizing prose poetry and high nonsense: “On a bimulous night...everything is strange-splendid and plum-purple...the trees eucalyptus back and forth, forth and back...in the fern-deep grove at the midnight end of the garden.” Cooney’s tranquil images complement the gently outlandish humor of the writing: hazy clouds, swaying tree branches, ripples on a pond, all bathed in an ethereal palette of indigo and lavender. #barbaracooney #night #sky #book #books #childrensbooks #beautifulbook #beautifulbooks #beautifulbookcover #beautifulbookcovers #picturebook #picturebooks #picturebookillustration #picturebookillustrations #picturebookart #classicchildrensbooks #childrensbookillustration #childrensliterature #illustration #kidlit #kidsbooks #bookworm #booklover #booklovers #bibliophile #bookstagram #bookstagrammer #bookstagrammers #instabook #instabooks
