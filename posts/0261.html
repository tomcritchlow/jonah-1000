Puss in Boots

by Giuliano Lunelli

ISBN 9781469213484 · Find on Bookshop.org

It’s time once again for a fairy tale theme week, and the story I’ll be looking at next is Puss in Boots! This first version comes to us from Giuliano Lunelli, who adopts an engaging and straightforward style while faithfully preserving the details of Charles Perrault’s tale “Le Maître Chat ou le Chat Botté” (“Master Cat, or the Cat in Boots”). The art exaggerates form and perspective to highlight key elements and emphasize power dynamics between the characters.
