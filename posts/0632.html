Molly Whuppie

by Walter de la Mare · illustrated by Errol Le Cain

ISBN 9780374350000 · Buy on Bookshop.org

Danger? Check. Suspense? Check. Good triumphing over evil? Check. The Bridge of One Hair? Check. These are just a few of the strange and wonderful elements of Walter de la Mare and Errol Le Cain’s Molly Whuppie. A young girl uses her cunning and bravery to extricate herself and her sisters from the clutches of a bloodthirsty giant. When they come to the house of a king, she negotiates smart matches for all three of them with the princes by brazenly stealing the giant’s magical treasures. De la Mare’s faithful retelling and Le Cain’s eye-popping visual aesthetic make for an unforgettable reading experience.
