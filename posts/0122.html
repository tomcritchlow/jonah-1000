#122 of 1001 · November 30, 2020

For a tale of action, chivalry, and heart, I recommend Sir Cedric by @roygerrard_archive. In this medieval-set original story, the eponymous knight leaves his post as castle lord and sets out in search of excitement. Along the way, he jousts against a wicked villain, rescues a damsel in distress, and inspires a people to defend themselves against an invading threat. Gerrard uses the form of a heroic ballad in common meter, with lines of lively anapestic meter begging to be read aloud.
