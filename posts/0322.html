The Night Riders

by Matt Furie

ISBN 9781936365562 · Find on Bookshop.org

Have you ever left home and wandered out into a magical night where anything could happen? The Night Riders, a wordless picture book by Matt Furie, depicts just such a marvelous journey. An anthropomorphic frog wakes up and takes a bicycle ride along with a faithful rat companion through a bizarre landscape teeming with eerie wildlife, including a large, fanged wyvern and a bat operating a subterranean computer console. Together, the motley crew dive underwater, resurface, and perch on a cliff top to watch the sun rise. Furie’s surreal art creates an atmosphere of enchantment and delight.
