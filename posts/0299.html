Ike's Incredible Ink

by Brianne Farley

ISBN 9780763671396 · Buy on Bookshop.org

For some unique tips on how to tackle writer’s block, take a peek at Ike’s Incredible Ink by @briannehfarley. After deciding to write a story, Ike finds himself stuck with no idea what to write. He soon concludes that in order to produce a truly top-notch work of literature he must manufacture his own ink. Ike mixes his ink from the darkest ingredients he can locate: shadows, the feathers of the Booga-bird, unknown material from the dark side of the moon (necessitating the construction of a rocket). Once finished, he sets to work and the inspiration begins to flow. Farley keeps her phrases short and simple, allowing the whimsical fantasy of the story to shine.
