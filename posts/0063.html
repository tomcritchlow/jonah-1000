The Dream Child

by David McPhail

ISBN 9780434949717 · Buy on Bookshop.org

I’m eager to share with you the soft and soothing energy of David McPhail’s The Dream Child. The eponymous character wanders without effort or urgency through a series of magical nighttime environments, her purpose in the story not to teach a lesson or to explain a natural phenomenon but simply to embody and honor the imaginative power of youth. McPhail’s text is formatted as a poem in free verse with luxuriant language and creative line breaks.
