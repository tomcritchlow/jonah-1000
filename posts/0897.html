As Little Red Riding Hood Sequel Week draws to a close, let us emerge into the light of a new world. I invite you to share with me the transcendent beauty of Little Red, a meditative and profound wordless picture book by @beatrizmartinvidal. The work is composed of twenty-four images, which are divided into three sequences of eight each. In “Going Red”, a young girl is caught in a whirlwind of red leaves, which swirl around her and weave themselves together to form a tight-fitting hood. “The Game of Questions” shows the girl examining a pallid white mask, which is soon revealed to be perched on the snout of a shadowy wolf. Finally, “Going Out” depicts the girl rising from within the wolf’s hairy hide and leaping into the air, blood-red flowers sprouting from the wound and bursting into bloom around her.
