See tomorrow’s post for more details… Jane Yolen and John Wallner explore the passage of time and the cycle of the year in Ring of Earth. A series of four sweeping poems explores the movements and sensations of creatures representing the seasons—the weasel for winter, the spring peeper for spring, the dragonfly for summer, and the goose for autumn. Yolen composes lyrical verse with subtly different rhyme schemes and metric patterns for each animal, and Wallner supplies breathtaking watercolor images of nature.
