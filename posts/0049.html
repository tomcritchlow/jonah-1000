The Mouldy

by William Mayne · illustrated by Nicola Bayley

ISBN 9780224020923 · Buy on Bookshop.org

A charming little thing indeed is The Mouldy, written by William Mayne and illustrated by Nicola Bayley. Mayne sets his tale “in the great garden of the world”, where an idyllic community of sentient flower beings suddenly finds itself threatened by a monster—the eponymous Mouldy, whose nature and motives prove not quite as villainous as they might appear.
