Today’s book celebrates the deep kindness and generosity of the human spirit: The Falling Stars, translated from German of the Brothers Grimm by Rosemary Lanning and illustrated by Eugen Sopko. A young girl, orphaned and homeless, sets out into the world with only a crust of bread and the clothes on her back, all of which she soon gives away to those in even greater need. Finally, when she is left with nothing at all, stars rain down from the sky and turn into gold coins, thus providing her with a fortune. Lanning’s appealing translation honors the Grimms’ language, while Sopko’s pictures, done in a naïve art style, ameliorate the profound sadness of the story’s setting.
