Cinderella

by Laura Francesca Filippucci

ISBN 9780141933818 · Find on Bookshop.org

For the next entry in my Cinderella series, it’s this version by Laura Francesca Filippucci. The setting features dainty half-timbered houses and handsome castle towers, while the characters are depicted with well-rendered facial expressions and body language. A note for folklore enthusiasts: Filippucci bases her retelling wholly on Aschenputtel, the variant collected by the Brothers Grimm in Kinder- und Hausmärchen—even including the grisly detail of the stepmother convincing the stepsisters to cut off parts of their feet in order to fit into the glass slipper.
