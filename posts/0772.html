Have nightmares ever troubled your sleep? If so, have a look at The Night Lion by @sannedufft. Morgan has an active imagination. With his hat and sword, he loves to embody the character of a brave knight. When the figure of a shadowy robber in his nightmare causes him to fear sleep, his Nana brings him a toy lion. The stuffed animal enters Morgan’s dreams as a living, breathing creature, and the two form an instant bond, stronger than any menacing apparition.
