Rapunzel

illustrated by Carol Heyer

ISBN 9780606259323 · Find on Bookshop.org

The next version of Rapunzel comes from illustrator @carolheyer, who renders the story in acrylic paintings on canvas, using live models for reference. The bright hues and glossy textures lend a vivid look to the retelling. Rapunzel’s tower stands as a lonely stone column with a conical roof. A note for folklore enthusiasts: The text (credited to Ideals Publishing Corporation) names the witch “Gothel” after her fairy/sorceress counterpart in the original Brothers Grimm tale, Frau Gothel (a name that roughly translates to “Dame Godmother”).
