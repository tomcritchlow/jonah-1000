One year ago this week, I began the first of my Fairy Tale Theme Weeks with an exploration of seven picture book versions of Cinderella. The experience was a good one, and I went on to do many more fairy tale sequences over the course of the year. To celebrate the anniversary of that very first project, I’m delighted to bring you a second Cinderella Week, with a completely new set of seven books! First is a lovely adaptation by Roberto Innocenti, who updates the story by setting it in interwar London.
