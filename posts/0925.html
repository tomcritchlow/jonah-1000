Caretakers of Wonder

by Cooper Edens

ISBN 9780881380880 · Find on Bookshop.org

Cooper Edens brings us the book of the day: Caretakers of Wonder. Have you ever asked yourself who might be working behind the scenes to imbue the world with magic? To put up new stars in the sky and keep them alight? To button up the night and fasten the sky to the horizon? To weave meadows? To put fruit on tree branches? To mend clouds? To raise up the sun and guide it safely down again? It is the mystical Caretakers of Wonder.
