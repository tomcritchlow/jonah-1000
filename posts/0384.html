The Firebird

by Robert D. San Souci · illustrated by Kris Waldherr

ISBN 9780803707993 · Find on Bookshop.org

Continuing into a new phase of Firebird Week, let us examine The Firebird by Robert D. San Souci and @kriswaldherr. After climbing into a mysterious garden, Prince Ivan is whisked into an epic conflict involving the wizard Kastchei, the captive Princess Elena, and the wondrous Firebird. San Souci crafts a strong and vibrant retelling, full of compelling action and powerful imagery. Waldherr illustrates the narrative with a series of oil paintings executed over golden acrylic underpaintings on stretched, gessoed, and sanded paper. In her interpretation of San Souci’s text (“Sometimes it seemed a bird, sometimes a woman”), she depicts the Firebird alternately as a bird and as a winged human woman with orange dress, skin, hair, and feathers. A note for folklore enthusiasts: this book retells the plot of the 1910 Russian ballet The Firebird, for which choreographer Mikhail Fokin and artist Alexandre Benois combined elements of several traditional tales to form a story honoring the breadth of Russian folk culture.
