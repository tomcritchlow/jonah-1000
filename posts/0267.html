Rounding out the week is this stunning adaptation of Puss in Boots by Fred Marcellino, who in both text and artwork brings forth a definitive vision of the story. The language of the story faithfully translates both the wording and intention of Perrault’s original, while the bright, smooth-textured illustrations capture the ornate decorative aesthetic of French baroque fashion. The majestic palace of Marcellino’s ogre projects an aura of refinement, subtly belied by the coiled snakes jutting out from under the cloche-covered platters carried by his servants.
