Masquerade

by Kit Williams

ISBN 9781925005844 · Find on Bookshop.org

And now for something completely different: Masquerade, an elaborate puzzle of a book designed, written, and illustrated by @kitwilliamsartist. The absurdist text and cryptic art function together as a riddle with a single valid solution pointing to the location of a real physical treasure actually buried by the author in secret prior to the book’s publication. Having sparked the interest of amateur sleuths around the world, the mystery was solved after a few years had passed...though the first searcher to identify the correct location was later revealed to have cheated through insider knowledge.
