The Great Quillow

by James Thurber · illustrated by Steven Kellogg

ISBN 9780156364904 · Find on Bookshop.org

For an uproarious good time, I heartily recommend James Thurber’s original tale The Great Quillow, illustrated by Steven Kellogg. A quiet town is threatened by the arrival of the terrible giant Hunder, who demands a daily tribute of three sheep, a pie baked from a thousand apples, a chocolate the size of a wagon wheel, and other extravagances that will soon beggar the community. Only Quillow the toymaker is clever enough to construct a plan to outwit the giant, beguiling him with nonsensical stories and gaslighting him until he flees in panic and throws himself into the sea.
