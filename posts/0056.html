The book of the day is Tom Thumb by Richard Jesse Watson. In this adaptation of a well-known fairy tale, a child is born the size of a thumb and a series of delightful adventures begins. Tom must keep his wits about him as he navigates a perilously oversized world of teacups and mousetraps, ravens and fish, giants and kings.
