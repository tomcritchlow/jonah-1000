Night Circus

by Etienne Delessert

ISBN 9781568462776 · Find on Bookshop.org

In Night Circus, Etienne Delessert presents a surreal sequence of images in the form of an odd circus train. While out walking his dog, the unnamed protagonist witnesses his own pet cat driving a racecar. Behind is attached a line of ten flatbeds carrying incredible things, some quietly beautiful (angels playing chess, an old-time Bluegrass fiddler, a snow globe filled with butterflies) and some outrageously bizarre (three clowns in the form of Franz Kafka, Samuel Beckett, and Eugene O’Neill, a pack of mad dogs performing music, three pigs slicing a wolf pie, an alligator getting its nails painted).
