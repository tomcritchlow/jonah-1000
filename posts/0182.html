Pezzetino

by Leo Lionni

ISBN 9780394831565 · Find on Bookshop.org

There’s a deceptive depth of philosophically thought-provoking material bundled into Pezzetino by Leo Lionni. The book presents us with an abstract geometric universe in which motley organisms composed of brightly colored square tiles roam around a barren landscape of marbled rock. Each is named for its characteristic behavior or environment: the one-who-runs, the strong-one, the swimming-one, the one-on-the-mountain, the flying-one, the wise-one. Far different is Pezzetino (Italian for “little piece”), a single orange square who concludes from his diminutive size that he must be a mislaid fragment of one of his larger compatriots. Seeing Pezzetino’s distress, the wise-one sends him on a quest to the Island of Wham, where he discovers the unexpected truth of his inner nature.
