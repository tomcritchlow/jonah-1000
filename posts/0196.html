Beyond the Pond

by Joseph Kuefler

ISBN 9780062364272 · Find on Bookshop.org

Dive into a world of play made real with the delightful Beyond the Pond by @josephkuefler. Ernest D. combats boredom by donning his scuba gear and swimming into the murky waters of his pond, accompanied only by his trusty dog. On the pond’s mysterious “other side” he finds dazzling vistas, ghastly beasts, and the thrill of the unknown. When he journeys back home, his house and its surrounding environs all seem a little less ordinary, a little more intriguing. Kuefler constructs a winningly cheerful protagonist in Ernest D., whose catchphrase (“Sensational!”) reflects both an adventurer’s unbridled enthusiasm and a naturalist’s sense of wonder.
