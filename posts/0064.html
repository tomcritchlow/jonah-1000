Mufaro’s Beautiful Daughters

by John Steptoe

ISBN 9780874994629 · Buy on Bookshop.org

Right now I’m revisiting the lovely Mufaro’s Beautiful Daughters by @johnlsteptoe. Though many publications, bookstores, and libraries classify it as a retelling of a traditional tale, the book is in fact an original story inspired by Xhosa folklore. The characters’ names are drawn from the Shona language and the elegant ink-and-watercolor illustrations are modeled on the landscape of Zimbabwe.
