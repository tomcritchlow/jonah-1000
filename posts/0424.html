Sleeping Beauty

by Jane Yolen · illustrated by Ruth Sanderson

ISBN 9780394554334 · Buy on Bookshop.org

Sleeping Beauty Week moves steadily ahead, and next up is a version by Jane Yolen and Ruth Sanderson. Yolen’s graceful prose draws readers irresistibly through the enchantment and dark drama of the story. Sanderson’s oil paintings, created from reference photos of costumed models, shine with rich colors and a striking contrast between light and shadow. A note for folklore enthusiasts: Yolen has adapted the plot from the Brothers Grimm, keeping the heroine’s name Briar Rose and the number of fairies at the royal feast thirteen. The messenger who informs the queen that she is with child, instead of a crab or frog, is a fairy as well.
