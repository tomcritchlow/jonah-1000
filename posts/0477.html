Six-Dinner Sid

by Inga Moore

ISBN 9780750030069 · Find on Bookshop.org

Here is Inga Moore’s irresistible Six-Dinner Sid, whose enterprising feline protagonist uses his wits to maintain a luxurious lifestyle. He receives warmth, care, and—most importantly—six dinners per day by posing as six different cats for six different owners, all living on the same block. After a brief illness and six trips to the veterinarian, Sid’s ruse is exposed and the six neighbors put him on a strict diet. Undaunted, he flees and relocates to a new street where six different owners who all know him as the same cat are happy to share him and—most importantly—give him six dinners every day.
