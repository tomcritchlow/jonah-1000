Here’s another childhood favorite of mine, this one quite well known: Rumpelstiltskin, retold and illustrated by the inordinately talented @paulozelinsky. A Caldecott honor book, this one was rightly celebrated upon its release for the rich detail and sheer grandeur of its art.
