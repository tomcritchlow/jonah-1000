Lost!

by David McPhail

ISBN 9780440844211 · Buy on Bookshop.org

The spirit of play and kindness can truly be felt in Lost! by David McPhail. A bear accidentally hitches a ride in the back of a delivery truck from the forest to the big city. A young boy makes it his mission to help the bear, and after a day of fun and adventure they successfully make their way out to the bear’s woodland habitat. However, now the boy is lost, so the bear sets out to help him in turn.
