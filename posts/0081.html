I give you an old-fashioned story of wit, pride, and romance: The Snow Rose by Sandra and Michel Laroche. In this charming early-modern-set tale, a gallant troubadour tackles a series of formidable puzzles in order to demonstrate his cleverness and earn the hand of a haughty princess in marriage. In between solving riddles, he spends his days in the company of an innkeeper’s shy and gentle daughter. As the seasons pass, the glamour of a royal match begins to fade alongside the appeal of deep affection and genuine companionship.
