Downpour

by Emily Martin · illustrated by Mara Shaugnessy

ISBN 9781620875452 · Buy on Bookshop.org

A familiar weather pattern is viewed through a dazzlingly unexpected lens in Downpour by Emily Martin and Mara Shaugnessy. A sudden burst of rain causes all the red to drip off the poppies in the field and wash over everything, staining plants, animals, and objects for miles around. Martin’s poetic text unfolds methodically, the gentle rhythm and loose rhyme scheme allowing for quiet contemplation.
