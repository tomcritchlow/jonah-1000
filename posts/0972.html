Night Lunch

by Eric Fan · illustrated by Dena Seiferling

ISBN 9780711289222 · Find on Bookshop.org

Imagine that you can’t sleep, so you look out the window and see a pack of friendly animals getting together for a midnight snack. You have just imagined yourself in the realm of Night Lunch, a cozy collaboration between @ericfanillustration and @dena.seiferling. In the middle of the night, as a full moon hangs in the air, a hungry group of nocturnal animals gathers on a brightly lit city street. With a clip-clop of hooves and a thinking bell, the night lunch cart rolls in. A gentle owl cooks tasty food to order: fried eggs for the moths, sausages and peppers for the bats, puddings for the possums. When the crowd has dispersed, a timid little mouse remains to sweep the gutter. The owl generously opens the door of the cart, and the two share a feast of tea and cakes before the first light of morning creeps over the horizon.
