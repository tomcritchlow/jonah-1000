Goodnight Moon

by Margaret Wise Brown · illustrated by Clement Hurd

ISBN 9780616015988 · Find on Bookshop.org

The book of the day is Goodnight Moon, the immortal classic by Margaret Wise Brown and Clement Hurd. Here is a cozy bedroom, with a child in the last few minutes before sleep watched over by an old lady whispering, “Hush” (both characters are depicted as anthropomorphic rabbits). Brown’s text achieves a hypnotically soothing rhythm as it bids goodnight to each object in the room, from kittens and mittens to a red balloon and, of course, the eponymous moon. Hurd’s art wonderfully complements the calming atmosphere, moving gradually from bright to dark with the turn of each page.
