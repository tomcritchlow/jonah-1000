Grandpa Green

by Lane Smith

ISBN 9788449445705 · Find on Bookshop.org

The book of the day is Lane Smith’s Grandpa Green. A young boy takes readers through the history of an unnamed, unseen man—eventually revealed to be his great-grandfather—from childhood to old age. Smith cleverly pairs his thoughtful text with images of the boy passing through a topiary garden, each sculpted hedge illustrating a key event or object from the old man’s experience; we thus encounter a life revisited and expressed through art.
