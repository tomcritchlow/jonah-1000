Noah’s Cats and the Devil’s Fire

by Arielle North Olson · illustrated by Barry Moser

ISBN 9780531085844 · Find on Bookshop.org

Check out Noah’s Cats and the Devil’s Fire, Arielle North Olson and Barry Moser’s adaptation of a Romanian folktale set during the biblical story of the flood. After a humiliating rebuff on the gangplank, the devil sneaks aboard the newly built ark disguised as a particularly wicked-looking mouse. When the malevolent fiend enacts a cunning plan to sink the sturdy vessel, it’s up to Noah’s trusty cats to thwart him once and for all.
