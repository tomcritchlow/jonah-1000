Draw Me a Star

by Eric Carle

ISBN 9780698116320 · Find on Bookshop.org

There’s a haunting beauty to Eric Carle’s Draw Me a Star, at once delicately simple and deeply complex. An artist, alone in a blank void, hears a mysterious voice asking him to draw a star. Each of his creations asks him for a new drawing, and so an entire world is born.
