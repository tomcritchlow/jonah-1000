The Twelve Wild Geese

by Matt Faulkner

ISBN 9780590456845 · Find on Bookshop.org

Brothers to Birds Week flies ever on, and today I’ve been reading Matt Faulkner’s adaptation of The Twelve Wild Geese. Faulkner creates a lively energy in his illustrations through loose, sketched lines and prominent cross-hatching, all overlaid on rich watercolor washes. His characters convey emotion through vivid facial expression and highly exaggerated anatomy—particularly the young king’s stepmother, a hulking figure who looms over the rest of the court with smirks and scowls, brandishing sharp talons.
