Where does creativity come from? Is it a conscious practice, an instinct that can be refined and mastered, or simply an innate gift? In his fantastical tale The Ink House, @rorydobner posits an explanation altogether more concrete. In the eponymous homestead, a long succession of artists, writers, poets, and composers have received miraculous inspiration from the enigmatic well of ink in a vaulted cistern below the floor. Once a year, the current owner of the house departs in a hot air balloon, off on a quest for marvelous treasures. Taking advantage of the empty space, a diverse group of anthropomorphic animals holds an annual gathering of raucous activity and entertainment. Dobner gifts his characters with alliterative names and over-the-top quirks and proclivities.
