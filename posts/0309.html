The book of the day is Ingri and Edgar Parin d’Aulaire’s The Two Cars, the gentle tale of a pair of sentient cars, one old and one new, who go for a drive to see which is the best car on the road. After several encounters with humans, animals, and other vehicles, they return to their garage with a deeper understanding of what makes a car truly great.
