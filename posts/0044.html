Albidaro and the Mischievous Dream

by Julius Lester · illustrated by Jerry Pinkney

ISBN 9780803719873 · Find on Bookshop.org

Here’s a little something for you: Albidaro and the Mischievous Dream, by Julius Lester and @jerrypinkneystudio. Nimbly leaping from the sublime to the ridiculous and back again, the story takes a look at a pair of celestial siblings and the increasingly strange effects of their power over Earth’s children and animals. Lester revels in the playful possibilities of his offbeat scenario, inventing words and exaggerating spellings with evident glee.
