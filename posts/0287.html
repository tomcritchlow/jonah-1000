Rapunzel

by Dorothée Duntze

ISBN 9780606259323 · Find on Bookshop.org

Rapunzel refracts through an altogether different lens in the eclectic vision of Dorothée Duntze. While the wondrous garden and the mother’s craving for leafy greens already accords the Grimms’ tale a strong horticultural theme, the design of the characters and environment here foregrounds flora’s influence on the plot. The mother’s skirt and petticoats resemble a the petals of a pink flower; Rapunzel’s dress, bulbous and green, gives off the appearance of a brussels sprout; the witch’s voluminous gown—shot through with veins, covered with snail and slugs, sporting an ever-present puddle with a frog on a lily pad—recalls a head of cabbage.
