The Hymn of the Sun

by St. Francis of Assisi · illustrated by Tony Wright

ISBN 9780930647483 · Find on Bookshop.org

How poetic, how meditative, how life-affirming, how hypnotically beautiful is this picture book version of The Hymn of the Sun by St. Francis of Assisi with illustrations by Tony Wright. Often called the Canticle of the Sun, or Laudes Creaturarum (“Praise of the Creatures”), the hymn is thought to have been written around the year 1224. St. Francis, who believed that commoners should be able to worship in a language they understood, composed it in his native Umbrian dialect of Italian. The text here comes from a 1927 English translation held in the Bodleian Library at Oxford.
