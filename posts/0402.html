Blueberries for the Queen

by John and Katherine Paterson · illustrated by Susan Jeffers

ISBN 9780066239439 · Find on Bookshop.org

For an appealing bit of historical fiction with more truth than meets the eye, pick up Blueberries for the Queen, written by husband and wife John and Katherine Paterson and illustrated by Susan Jeffers. William, a child living in Massachusetts during World War II, dearly wishes he were old enough to contribute to the war effort. When he learns that Queen Wilhelmina of the Netherlands has brought her family to America for safety and rented a nearby estate for the summer, he resolves to meet her. After gathering a basket of blueberries from his family’s field, he brings it to the grand house and presents it to the queen—an act he terms “peace work”. Jeffers renders William’s view of the world by depicting his extraordinary dreams, his moments of imagination, and his view of real events through a fantasy lens.
