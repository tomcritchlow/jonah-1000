Smon Smon

by Sonia Danowski

ISBN 9780735843073 · Find on Bookshop.org

Today I am basking in the gentle strangeness of Sonia Danowski’s Smon Smon. The book takes place on Gon Gon, an alien world populated by bizarre yet recognizable flora—pon pons, lon lons, ron rons—and fauna—Klon Klons and Flon Flons.
