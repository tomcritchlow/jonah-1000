The Grey Lady and the Strawberry Snatcher

by Molly Bang

ISBN 9780590075473 · Buy on Bookshop.org

And now for something deeply strange and deeply special: The Grey Lady and the Strawberry Snatcher by Molly Bang, a wordless exercise in pure suspense. A woman buys a basket of strawberries at the market and sets off for home, but soon finds herself pursued by...well, a being that can only be described as a Strawberry Snatcher. The chase moves out of town as the Grey Lady flees by bus, wades through a lonely swamp, climbs an enormous tree, swings on a hanging vine, and finally disappears into an unearthly void, all with the Strawberry Snatcher hot on her heels.
