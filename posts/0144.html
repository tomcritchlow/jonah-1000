One of the few truly universal experiences must surely be caring for a crying baby at night. In Niki Daly’s Mary Malloy and the Baby Who Wouldn’t Sleep, a young girl finds herself in just such a predicament. Mary Malloy has been kept awake until late into the night by Baby (suggested to be a younger sibling), who simply cannot be calmed. The crescent moon floats down and offers to take over, but promptly kidnaps the infant and floats away. Fortunately, a resourceful crocodile tricks the villainous moon into dropping Baby and returns the infant safely to Mary’s arms.
