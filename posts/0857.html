Snow White

by Jennifer Greenway · illustrated by Erin Augenstine

ISBN 9780836249064 · Buy on Bookshop.org

As we gaze at the mysteries of our own reflections, let us add another title to this Sequence of Seven Snow Whites with a version by Jennifer Greenway and Erin Augenstine. The gentle text is complemented well by the emotive illustrations. In particular, the spirit of the queen’s looking glass is given a remarkable degree of visual characterization for the brevity of its appearance. Carved into the mirror frame itself, it leers downward with a wicked grin.
