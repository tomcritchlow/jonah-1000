The Tunnel

by Anthony Browne

ISBN 9789681639716 · Buy on Bookshop.org

It’s October, and the autumn night brings a chill both eerie and comfortingly familiar. I’ve decided to celebrate the week leading up to Halloween by posting a scary book each day. To begin, here’s Anthony Browne’s The Tunnel, in which a brother and sister, thrown out of the house for misbehavior, discover a mysterious passageway leading to...well, who knows where? The plot hinges upon their decision to crawl inside, the ominous tableau of the darkly gaping tunnel mouth a visual fulcrum for the narrative. Browne mines the visit to a frightening otherworld (whether real or imagined) for the progress it affords the siblings’ emotional relationship.
