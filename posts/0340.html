Even when one is surrounded by opportunities, one can still be bored. Even when one is all alone, one can still create entire universes of wonder. For a perfect example, see Zephyr Takes Flight by Steve Light. Sent to her room for making a pest of herself with her toy airplanes, a young girl peers behind her dresser and discovers a mysterious door leading to a hangar filled with exotic aircraft. She climbs aboard a multi-tiered dirigible and soars off to a pastoral landscape populated by convivial winged pigs. Light’s short, punchy sentences and warm-hued, loosely drawn art combine for a merry romp.
