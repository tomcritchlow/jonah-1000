Free Fall

by David Wiesner

ISBN 9789573211969 · Find on Bookshop.org

And now for something I’ve never featured in a post before: a wordless picture book. If you’ve never read one, you might not be aware of this unique format in which an illustrator tells a story through pictures alone, rather like the literary equivalent of a silent film. This is Free Fall, created by the sensational David Wiesner, and it’s a true feast for the imagination. We follow a young boy as he drifts off to sleep and finds himself traversing an extraordinary dream world. The characters, the objects, and the terrain itself seem fundamentally unstable and impermanent: chess pieces transform into royal courtiers, a jagged wall morphs into a menacing dragon, a skyline of buildings collapses to reveal the fluttering pages of an atlas.
