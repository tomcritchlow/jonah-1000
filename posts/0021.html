Today’s book is Count Silvernose, an Italian fairy tale adapted by Erik A. Kimmel and illustrated by @omarrayyanart. Unusually for a fairy tale, Kimmel depicts his heroine as large, physically powerful, hardworking, talented, and clever—but expressly not beautiful. Fascinatingly, Rayyan based his splendid paintings on art from a sixteenth-century Italian sketchbook.
