This is A Weave of Words, an Armenian folktale retold by Robert D. San Souci and illustrated by @raul.colon.7140. San Souci creates an admirable hero and heroine who forge a touching bond based on knowledge and mutual respect. The iconic image here is certainly the dev, a monster found throughout the folk tradition of Armenia and other Middle Eastern nations.
