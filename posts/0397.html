The book of the day is Dennis Nolan’s Dinosaur Dream. When Wilbur hears a tapping noise at his window, he finds a baby dinosaur outside. He names it Gideon (after Gideon Mantell, discoverer of the first dinosaur fossil), and resolves to guide it safely home. Together, boy and beast set out on a walk that takes them not across a physical space but back through time.
