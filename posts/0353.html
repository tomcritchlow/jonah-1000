Beauty and the Beast

by An Leysen

ISBN 9781605372518 · Find on Bookshop.org

Here is another entry in Beauty and the Beast Week: an enchanting adaptation by An Leysen, whose straightforward writing style and dainty character design provide a diverting and accessible entertainment. Leysen makes several pragmatic changes to the story: she names the protagonist “Belle” (the untranslated French form of “Beauty”), she collapses the two cruel older sisters into a singe character, and she opts to begin the story with the backstory of the Beast, cursed as a young prince by an enchantress not for any particular infraction toward her but simply for his broad personality flaws.
