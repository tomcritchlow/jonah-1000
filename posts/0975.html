East of the Sun & West of the Moon

by László Gál

ISBN 9780613011679 · Buy on Bookshop.org

Nearing the end of my thousand picture book posts, I cannot help but squeeze in one last Fairy Tale Theme Week. This one is devoted to my very favorite tale type, in which a determined heroine must save her lover from a magical curse—The Search for the Lost Husband. First up, László Gál supplies a handsome adaptation of East of the Sun & West of the Moon. A young girl agrees to marry a mysterious white bear in exchange for a gift of enormous wealth for her family. When she discovers that he is a human man under a terrible curse, she must follow him across the world to a castle east of the sun and west of the moon and rescue him from the clutches of a pack of hideous trolls. Gál’s elegant retelling and delightful drawings create an air of wonder and enchantment.
