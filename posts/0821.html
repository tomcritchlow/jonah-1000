A Woggle of Witches

by Adrienne Adams

ISBN 9781534412460 · Find on Bookshop.org

We stand on the verge of Halloween: the precipice of darkness. Let Adrienne Adams provide a gleam of good cheer with her charming book A Woggle of Witches. The full moon rises over a dense forest. A sleepy pack of witches arises and prepares for revelry. They sit at a long banquet table for a merry feast, and then they mount their broomsticks and soar into the air. After a brief rest stop on the moon, they touch down in a cornfield. Suddenly, a parade of trick-or-treaters happens by, sending the witches scurrying to hide behind a few oversized pumpkins. Once the coast is clear, the frightened coven returns safely home.
