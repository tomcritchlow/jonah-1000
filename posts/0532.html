May the Stars Drip Down

by Jeremy Chatelain · illustrated by Nikki McClure

ISBN 9781419710247 · Find on Bookshop.org

Here is a book to bring on sweet and nourishing sleep: @jeremy.chatelain and @nikkimcclure’s May the Stars Drip Down. The text, taken from the lyrics of a lullaby by Chatelain’s band Cub Country, forms a soothing prayer for a sleeping child to be embraced and protected by the physical elements of nature.
