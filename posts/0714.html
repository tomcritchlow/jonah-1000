The Moon Jumpers

by Janice May Udry · illustrated by Maurice Sendak

ISBN 9780060261467 · Buy on Bookshop.org

There’s something magical about the twilight hours on a summer evening. The hazy warmth, the soft sounds, the slowly dimming light…all these sensations and more are captured in Janice May Udry and Maurice Sendak’s The Moon Jumpers. Four children head outside after dinner to run around and tumble in the yard around the house. Reveling in the dusk, they sing, dance, play tag, climb trees, and tell ghost stories, watched all the while by a curious pet cat. By the time they are called inside again, they have named themselves the Moon Jumpers. They say goodnight to the moon, their parents, and each other before dropping off to sleep.
