In need of a heartwarming adventure with a dash of magic? Well, I have just the thing: Laura Krauss Melmed and Jim LaMarche’s The Rainbabies. An elderly couple discover to their astonishment that a mystical moonshower has left behind a dozen miraculous babies, each contained in a drop of dew on the meadow grass. Together, the husband and wife must protect their minuscule charges from a host of natural disasters until the infants’ provenance is revealed and all is set right.
