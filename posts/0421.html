The Witches of Venice

by Beni Montresor

ISBN 9780385263559 · Find on Bookshop.org

For a lovely bit of fantasy laced with excitement, I suggest you read Beni Montresor’s The Witches of Venice. A childless king and queen receive a magical plant from two Fairies of the Lagoon that blooms into a little boy. The king, unwilling to accept this bizarre phenomenon, insists that the boy is in fact a plant and must remain in the garden. After overhearing that the Witches of the Grand Canal have imprisoned a flower-plant girl, the boy builds a pigeon out of stray tree branches and rides off on the Wind to rescue her from their sinister palace.
