It Was a Dark and Stormy Night

by Allan Ahlberg · illustrated by Janet Ahlberg

ISBN 9780140555547 · Find on Bookshop.org

An idiosyncratic narrative device lies at the heart of It Was a Dark and Stormy Night by wife-and-husband team Janet and Allan Ahlberg. Young Antonio has been kidnapped by a band of rough brigands who demand he tell them a story. He obliges, but inserts the outlaws themselves into his tale, confronting them with a host of outrageous fictional hazards and indignities, including wolves, sharks, pirates, foul weather, mutiny, and—most horrifying of all—a Thingy. The bandits’ futile attempts to steer the plot in a favorable direction prove no match for the stress of Antonio's imaginary terrors and insults, which wind up completely derailing their villainous scheme.
