Greyling

by Jane Yolen · illustrated by William Stobbs

ISBN 9780399222627 · Find on Bookshop.org

The book of the day is Greyling by Jane Yolen and William Stobbs. A fisherman rescues a seal pup stranded on a sand bar. When he brings it home to his wife, they find it has transformed into a baby boy with grey eyes and grey hair. They name him Greyling and raise him as their own son. Fifteen years later, a terrible ocean storm swallows the family’s hut, and the fisherman is nearly drowned offshore. Greyling, in reality a selchie or seal spirit, selflessly dives into the water, reverts to his aquatic form, and rescues his father. He then vanishes from his parents’ lives, returning only for a brief visit one night out of each year. Yolen’s arresting prose injects this tradisitonal Shetland Islands tale with poetic language and high drama.
