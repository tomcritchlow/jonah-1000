Puss in Boots

by Lincoln Kirstein · illustrated by Alain Vaës

ISBN 9780785799559 · Find on Bookshop.org

If you want a fairy tale with beauty, wit, and excitement, then look no further: here’s a grand adaptation of Charles Perrault’s Puss in Boots by Lincoln Kirstein and @vaesart. To his retelling of the familiar story of the clever cat using his wits to amass wealth and prestige for his ineffectual master, Kirstein adds alluring details and gripping action in key moments.
