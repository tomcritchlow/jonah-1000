The Heart of the Wood

by Marguerite W. Davol · illustrated by Sheila Hamanaka

ISBN 9780671747787 · Find on Bookshop.org

Here is Marguerite W. Davol and Sheila Hamanaka’s The Heart of the Wood, a gentle, soothing read that celebrates music, community, and joy. Davol’s poetic text, an adaptation of the cumulative nursery rhyme This Is the House That Jack Built, follows a piece of wood from its home in nature to a sawmill, to a workshop, and finally, in a new form, back to the forest.
