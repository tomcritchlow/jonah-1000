The Stolen Mirror

by Lidia Postma

ISBN 9781637997635 · Buy on Bookshop.org

If you’d like a wholly original story full of wondrous creatures, enchanted artifacts, and deep emotions, Lidia Postma has just the thing: an elaborate magical quest into another world. Here, for your viewing pleasure, is The Stolen Mirror. Michael has a secret that he has never revealed to anyone—his garden is home to a group of little elves and fairies. When his mother and father tell him that he will soon have a new baby sibling, he feels ambivalent and says he would rather have a bicycle. His parents obliging gift him a bicycle, which he enjoys pedaling all around town. One day, while he is out for a ride, the little folk appear and lead him through an old archway into a strange land. There, a morose tribe of people laments the loss of a marvelous mirror that tells viewers who they truly are. Michael just recover the mirror from a fierce and powerful dragon with the help of three magical birds. Afterward, he returns home, eager to meet his younger brother or sister for the first time.
