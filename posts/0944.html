Arnold of the Ducks

by Mordicai Gerstein

ISBN 9780060220020 · Find on Bookshop.org

Let Mordicai Gerstein amuse and delight you with his fanciful original story Arnold of the Ducks. A baby boy is carried out of his inflatable swimming pool by a pelican. After being deposited in he wilderness, he is found and raised by ducks. Eventually, he returns to his parents, but his heart will always remain with his wild avian family.
