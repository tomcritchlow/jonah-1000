Shadow

ISBN 9781442450592 · Find on Bookshop.org

Come read Shadow by Marcia Brown and be transported to a landscape of ideas and evocative imagery. Brown’s text translates a French-language poem by Blaise Cendrars called La Féticheuse (the Witch-Doctor). Here Shadow is an entity at once mysterious and familiar—a spirit in the forest, a presence dancing around the fire, a trickster just over one’s shoulder in everyday life.
