In the Haunted House

by Eve Bunting · illustrated by Susan Meddaugh

ISBN 9780785737155 · Find on Bookshop.org

Once again October has cast its delightfully dark shadow upon the landscape of my imagination. As I did last year, I’ll be counting down the seven days leading up to Halloween with a sequence of scary picture books. This first one, Eve Bunting and Susan Meddaugh’s In the Haunted House, begins with an austere Victorian home surrounded by a grove of barren trees. Bunting’s energetic rhyming couplets beckon us inside to meet a delightful cadre of fearsome creatures: ghosts, witches, bats, a mummy, a skeleton, a vampire, a werewolf, Frankenstein’s monster, and other ghouls and creepy-crawlies. Meddaugh’s spooky pencil-and-watercolor art depicts only the feet of the two humans taking their tour of the eerie house. In brief interludes throughout, the adult comforts the child and offers to leave early.
