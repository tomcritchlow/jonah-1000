#427 of 1001 · October 1, 2021

Sleeping Beauty

by Adèle Geras · illustrated by Christian Birmingham

ISBN 9780439581806 · Buy on Bookshop.org

Onward we press through Sleeping Beauty Week, though examining and contrasting this many versions of a single story can feel as difficult as struggling through a snarl of thorny briars. Today we have a handsome adaptation by @adelegeras and Christian Birmingham. Geras adds a good deal of flavor and intrigue to the traditional tale, fleshing out the plot with lyrical descriptive language to create her own singular vision, while Birmingham’s dazzling art brings a lush visual drama to the proceedings. A note for folklore enthusiasts: the wicked fairy takes a place of unusual prominence in this version, with Geras bestowing on her the original name Skura. Here she keeps bats, ravens, and wolves as familiars, and she appears to possess the power to turn into a little gray cat.
