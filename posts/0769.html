The Fantastic Adventures of Baron Munchausen

by Heinz Janisch · illustrated by Aljoscha Blau

ISBN 9781592700912 · Buy on Bookshop.org

I’m relishing the madcap tall tales of Heinz Janisch and Aljoscha Blau’s The Fantastic Adventures of Baron Munchausen. Delightfully weird incidents include a horse snagged on a church steeple, a flurry of goose feathers, a ride on a cannonball, pulling oneself upward by the hair in midair, running in place to rotate the earth beneath one’s feet, a sack full of sunshine, and an orchestral concert played in the belly of a whale.
