The Golden Bird

by Neil Philip · illustrated by Isabel Brent

ISBN 9780316705226 · Find on Bookshop.org

Continuing with my new Firebird Week, Neil Philip and Isabel Brent create a dazzling adventure in The Golden Bird. A young prince must discover who is eating the precious apples of his father, the king. So begins a tale of magic and danger—the prince retrieves a golden bird, a golden horse, and a beautiful princess, all with the help of a clever fox. Philip’s faithful adaptation is enhanced by Brent’s gilded art. A note for folklore enthusiasts: This tale was first published by the Brothers Grimm in Kinder- und Hausmärchen (1812).
