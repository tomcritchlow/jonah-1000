Here is a look at Edward Lear’s comic verse The Jumblies, with illustrations by Edward Gorey. The poem tells an epic story of seafaring adventure. A group of young explorers called the Jumblies set out across the ocean in a sieve. They sail here and there until they come a land all covered with trees, where they make many delightful purchases—a cranberry tart, a hive of silvery bees, a lovely monkey with lollipop paws, and many more. Finally, they sail back home and are welcomed by their community with open arms.
