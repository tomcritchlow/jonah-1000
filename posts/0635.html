The Gingerbread Man

by Eric A. Kimmel · illustrated by Megan Lloyd

ISBN 9781591127857 · Buy on Bookshop.org

Here’s a look at a classic fairy tale: Eric A. Kimmel and Megan Lloyd’s The Gingerbread Man. An elderly couple decide to bake some gingerbread. They cut the dough in the shape of a little man and put it in the oven. To their surprise, once he is decorated, the gingerbread man jumps off the table and runs away, leading them on a merry wild goose chase. The living cookie flees from a pig, a dog, a horse, and a cow before meeting his end in the jaws of a sly, hungry fox.
