Lift

by Minh Lê · illustrated by Dan Santat

ISBN 9780316703987 · Buy on Bookshop.org

Step through a seemingly ordinary doorway into an entirely new reality with @bottomshelfbks and @dsantat’s Lift. Young Iris loves to push the button when her family rides in their building’s elevator, considering it an important job. One day, disaster strikes: her little brother presses the button instead. Even worse, their parents praise him for it. Later, Iris finds a discarded elevator button set in a wall plate and tapes it up in her bedroom. To her surprise, the button transforms her closet door into a portal leading to far-off lands. Finally, it would seem, she has a means to escape her pesky younger sibling. But she soon realizes that a good adventure is far more fun with a sidekick in tow.
