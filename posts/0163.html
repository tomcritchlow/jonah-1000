King Bidgood’s in the Bathtub

by Audrey Wood · illustrated by Don Wood

ISBN 9789572089187 · Find on Bookshop.org

For a madcap farce illustrated with serious formalistic grandeur, I highly recommend King Bidgood’s in the Bathtub by @audreywoodauthor and Don Wood. The royal court is faced with an unexpected and ridiculous quandary: King Bidgood simply refuses to vacate the bath. No matter who puts forth a compelling reason to leave the tub—the Knight calls for a battle, the Queen offers lunch, the Duke proposes to go fishing—the playful monarch simply invites them in for an aquatic version of their desired activity. At last a seemingly insignificant member of the court devises a solution as hilarious as the problem itself.
