The Bone Man

by Laura Simms · illustrated by Michael McCurdy

ISBN 9780786820740 · Find on Bookshop.org

You’ll shiver indeed at The Bone Man, a frightening story by Laura Simms and Michael McCurdy. Young Nulwee and his grandmother live in a drought-stricken land. The last survivors of their tribe, they eke out a meager existence by gathering berries. One day, the boy accidentally awakens an ancient evil: Kokolimalayas, the Bone Man. Nulwee must hone his skill with a bow and arrow to defeat the enormous skeletal monster and restore peace to the land around him. Simms weaves a chilling narrative, enhanced by McCurdy’s eerie illustrations.
