Children of the Forest

by Elsa Beskow

ISBN 9789163831058 · Find on Bookshop.org

For a classic work that celebrates the rhythm and harmony of nature, check out Children of the Forest by Elsa Beskow. The book tells the story of a family living in a pine tree deep in the woods. These are forest people: a mother, a father, and their four children, Tom, Harriet, Sam, and Daisy.
