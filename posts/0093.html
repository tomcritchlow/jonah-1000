Anno’s Medieval World

by Mitsumasa Anno

ISBN 9788426121745 · Find on Bookshop.org

Though my personal taste clearly tends toward worlds of make-believe, occasionally a nonfiction volume grabs my fancy. Here, then, is Anno’s Medieval World by Mitsumasa Anno, adapted from the translation by Ursula Synge. The author lays out a broad and engaging history of the Middle Ages, relating the incremental advance of human knowledge through the centuries. Anno focuses particularly on the influence of folk belief on everyday life and the struggle of science to overcome superstition.
