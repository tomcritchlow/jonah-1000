For a diverting tale of gentle fantasy, check out Brian Wilcox and Lawrence David’s Full Moon. In a first-person narrative, a young boy relates a marvelous occurrence: a mystical globe given to him by his grandmother begins to levitate in his bedroom after dark. He lassoes the globe and is pulled out his bedroom window, across the sky, and to the city of New York, where he meets his grandmother for a birthday party atop the Statue of Liberty.
