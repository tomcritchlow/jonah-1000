As Halloween draws nearer, the whistle of the wind and the glint of the Hunter’s Moon often send me scurrying to curl up under a warm blanket and shiver with delight at a good ghost story. And what a ghost story I have here: The Monster and the Tailor by Paul Galdone. Based on The Sprightly Tailor, a Scottish folktale collected by Joseph Jacobs, this book is a visual master class in suspense. A tailor accepts an idiosyncratic commission from the Grand Duke to sew a pair of trousers after sundown in a haunted churchyard. As he works, an enormous ghoul begins to pull itself out of the ground, impishly presenting one giant body part at a time; the tailor must stitch with all his speed and skill in order to finish the garment and flee before the monster emerges.
