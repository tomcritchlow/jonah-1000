The Devil & Mother Crump

by Valerie Scho Carey · illustrated by Arnold Lobel

ISBN 9780060209827 · Buy on Bookshop.org

For a wickedly comedic caper, try The Devil & Mother Crump by Valerie Scho Carey and Arnold Lobel. An ill-tempered old baker woman named Mother Crump treats all around her with deep hostility. So disagreeable is she that she acquires a reputation for being “meaner than the devil”. When Lucifer himself hears this rumor, he comes to investigate, but he is quickly overpowered. To assuage Mother Crump, he grants her three wishes. She asks for the power to trap anyone who touches her flour barrel, her bread dough, or her birch tree. Armed with this malicious magic, she manages to overcome Death and all the Devils that come to try to collect her soul. Carey constructs a merry text full of slapstick misadventures; Lobel gives us devils and imps with equal parts humor and menace.
