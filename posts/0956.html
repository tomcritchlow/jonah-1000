Whuppity Stoorie

by Carolyn White · illustrated by S. D. Schindler

ISBN 9780399229039 · Find on Bookshop.org

Carolyn White and S. D. Schindler put their own twist on an old tale with their adaptation of Whuppity Stoorie. White’s expressive text is complemented by Schindler’s mixed-media art, rendered in watercolor, gouache, and pastels on colored mat board. A note for folklore enthusiasts: This story was first collected by Robert Chambers in his 1858 book Popular Rhymes of Scotland. White puts her own stamp on the plot by changing the baby to a young girl called Kate, naming the pig “Grumphie”, and having the mysterious green fairy travel in a whirlwind that makes the sound “Whuppity!
