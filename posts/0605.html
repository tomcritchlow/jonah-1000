Outside In

by Deborah Underwood · illustrated by Cindy Derby

ISBN 9788412314403 · Find on Bookshop.org

@underwoodwriter and @cindyderby’s Outside In may well inspire readers to find time to step out of their enclosures and open themselves to new (or perhaps old) experiences. A lament for our lost connection to the environment soon gives way to an optimistic exploration of the subtle ways we maintain physical and spiritual connection to the world around us.
