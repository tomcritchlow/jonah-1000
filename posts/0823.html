@katehoefler and @imageblockstudio regard both the macrocosmic and the microcosmic elements of our world, celebrating the connections between them, in their grand collaboration Great Big Things. A small mouse makes its way across a wide, sweeping terrain. Clutching a morsel of food, the enterprising little rodent traverses broad forests, deep oceans, and snowcapped mountains, all to bring a little bit of food to an eagerly waiting loved one. Hoefler’s stripped-down text drifts in and out of engaging rhythm and rhyme in pursuit of tender emotion.
