Let Stella Blackstone and @nicolettaceccoli’s An Island in the Sun whisk you away to a far-off ocean paradise and back again. A young boy sails in a little boat over a shimmering sea. On and on he travels, looking now and then through his spyglass to see what is on the horizon. Finally, he reaches a tropical island, where a beloved canine companion awaits. The two of them enjoy a beautiful day together before sailing back into the sunset. Blackstone unfolds the front half of the story via cumulative text, the first seven two-page spreads beginning with “I spy with my little eye” and adding a new element to the beginning of the list.
