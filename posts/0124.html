A delightful trove of nonsense awaits you in this version of Lewis Carroll’s irresistibly bizarre poem The Mad Gardener’s Song, illustrated by Sean Morrison. In each stanza, the eccentric title character blithely mistakes a human, animal, object, or concept for a rhyming (but otherwise hilariously contrasting) counterpart.
