Gentle Readers, my Final Ten Books continue with an instructional guide for a seemingly impossible task: How to Make a Bird by @megmckinlay and @mattottleyart. The text comprises a pensive set of materials and instructions. Readers are advised that they will need tiny, hollow bones, feathers, and a swiftly beating heart. McKinlay mines stanza after stanza of soaring prose poetry from her simple premise, investigating human experiences and emotions with a thoughtful eye.
