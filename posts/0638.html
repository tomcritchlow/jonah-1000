The Troll Music

by Anita Lobel

ISBN 9780060239305 · Find on Bookshop.org

There’s a dollop of mayhem and a light streak of the supernatural afoot in The Troll Music by Anita Lobel. A traveling band makes a living moving from town to town and playing songs for the people. One night, while the musicians are sleeping in the forest, a little troll attempts to rouse them for a tune; when they slumber on, the vengeful creature casts a spell that causes their instruments to produce animal noises. The troubadours must work together to find the troll and convince him to remove the spiteful enchantment.
