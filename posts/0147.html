The Snowman

by Raymond Briggs

ISBN 9780241352410 · Find on Bookshop.org

Christmas has arrived. I hope that everyone has been able to spend this day in comfort and warmth, surrounded by their loved ones. As I perused my bookshelves today, my eye lit on The Snowman by Raymond Briggs. In this wordless picture book, a young boy heads outside on a cold winter’s day to build a snowman. That night, to his surprise, his creation comes to life, and the two of them spend a magical evening together exploring the house and then flying across the sky. Briggs’ soft pencil drawings, laid out in comic-strip-style grids, neatly evoke action and emotion. The unforgettable final image, utterly heart-wrenching yet in retrospect inevitable, is surely one of the purest and saddest endings in the history of children’s literature.
