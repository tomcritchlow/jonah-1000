Nibble Nibble Mousekin

by Joan Walsh Anglund

ISBN 9780001932531 · Buy on Bookshop.org

May you draw on the power of Hansel and Gretel to push your greatest obstacles into the fire and let them burn away to ash. For today’s adaptation, I have Joan Walsh Anglund’s Nibble Nibble Mousekin. Alternating between black and white and full color art, Anglund puts a distinctive stamp on the tale. The two young children here are rendered as doll-like cherubs with dot eyes and blank faces. They meet their diametric opposite in the two antagonists—their stepmother and the witch, both charismatic figures with bony frames, pointy noses, and wicked, smirking expressions. A note for folklore enthusiasts: The unusual title here draws from a specific passage in the Brothers Grimm tale.
