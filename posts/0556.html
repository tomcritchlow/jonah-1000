Sidewalk Flowers

by JonArno Lawson · illustrated by Sydney Smith

ISBN 9788574067568 · Find on Bookshop.org

For children, found objects carry a distinct meaning, a subtle power that we all too often forget by the time we reach adulthood. Come and jog your memory with the wordless Sidewalk Flowers by JonArno Lawson and @sydneydraws. On the walk home from grocery shopping, a girl stops to pick flowers she notices growing here and there in little cracks and crevices of the urban environment. Eventually, she amasses a small, variegated bouquet. Though she enjoys her collection, she then begins leaving the flowers as gifts and offerings for those she passes: a dead bird, a man sleeping on a park bench, a dog on a leash, and finally her mother and younger siblings. The gentle, introspective story, conceived by Lawson, explores the meaning of childhood rituals and celebrates beauty for beauty’s sake.
