This is The Sorcerer’s Apprentice by Inga Moore. The story comes to us from an eighteenth-century poem by Johann Wolfgang von Goethe, but the tale type and its associated motifs date back to the ancient world. Moore’s rendition sizzles with enchantment: stone chimeras, flasks of alchemic liquid, and tomes of forbidden knowledge abound.
