Houses Floating Home

by Einar Turkowski

ISBN 9781592701834 · Find on Bookshop.org

There’s a quiet thread of magic and mystery woven throughout Einar Turkowski’s Houses Floating Home. The book does not feature a traditional continuous narrative. Rather, each two-page spread presents a pair of opposite words (translated from German by Belinda Cooper) and an illustration showing a surreal landscape in which the two concepts contrast, collide, and combine into wondrous forms.
