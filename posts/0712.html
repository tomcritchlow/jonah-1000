The Fool and the Fish

by Lenny Hort · illustrated by Gennady Spirin

ISBN 9780803708617 · Find on Bookshop.org

Please enjoy this elegant version of the fairy tale The Fool and the Fish, retold by Lenny Hort and illustrated by Gennady Spirin. Ivan, known to his neighbors as a lazy young man, encounters a fish that promises to grant his every wish. After a series of misadventures, he finds himself possessed of fabulous wealth and wed to a lovely princess. Spirin’s luxurious illustrations ably convey the grandeur and magic of the tale.
