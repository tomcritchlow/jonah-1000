Monster Mama

by Liz Rosenberg · illustrated by Stephen Gammell

ISBN 9780399219894 · Buy on Bookshop.org

Oh, dear. I’m in love with the electrifying strangeness of Liz Rosenberg and Stephen Gammell’s Monster Mama. The book is a bundle of contradictions: faintly sinister yet oddly comforting, shot through with garish horror yet tenderly affirming of otherness. Rosenberg affectionately introduces us to young Patrick Edward and his mother, a hulking, bestial figure whose bizarre appearance and frightening temperament relegate the two to a liminal existence: they dwell on the outskirts of the neighborhood, he mingling with the community while she hides in a cave at the back of the house. Ultimately, monstrousness is positioned as a unique and worthy trait that unites mother and son in a fiercely loving bond.
