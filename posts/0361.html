For an ode to the passing of the seasons, try The Story of the Root-Children by Sibylle von Olfers. In the cozy underground home of Mother Earth lie the Root-Children: a snowdrop, a forget-me-not, a buttercup, a daisy, and a poppy, and many others. After a long winter, they awaken and begin preparations for the arrival of spring.
