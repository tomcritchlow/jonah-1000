What do you do when you can’t fall asleep? Do you toss and turn? Do you stare at the ceiling? Does your mind begin to wander? This liminal moment is the subject of Marc Sutherland’s The Waiting Place. A child’s voice narrates in first-person rhyming verse the feeling of wakefulness while lying in bed at night.
