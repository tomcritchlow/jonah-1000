Little Red Riding Hood

by Trina Schart Hyman

ISBN 9780823406531 · Find on Bookshop.org

It’s Little Red Riding Hood Week, and today I have for you a classic version by Trina Schart Hyman. The details here are drawn faithfully from the Brothers Grimm variant of the tale, with the slight addition of a specific given name (“Elisabeth”) that precedes the heroine’s well-known nickname, and with the notable omission of the more gruesome details of the wolf’s demise. Hyman depicts a wealth of detail in her household and forest environments, and the borders on alternating pages contain floral designs and quilted patterns that evoke a homespun folk quality. A note for folklore enthusiasts: though working from the text of the Brothers Grimm, Hyman has titled her work “Little Red Riding Hood”, the common translation of Charles Perrault’s “Le Petit Chaperon Rouge”.
