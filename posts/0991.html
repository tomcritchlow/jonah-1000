Everything You Need for a Treehouse

by Carter Higgins · illustrated by Emily Hughes

ISBN 9781452153575 · Buy on Bookshop.org

There’s something magical about a treehouse—halfway between earth and sky, it stands as a symbol for friendship, relaxation, and imaginative play. @carterhiggins and @plaidemily have taken these elements and woven them into the delightful Everything You Need for a Treehouse. For construction, the list contains such sensible items as blueprints, hammers, nails, saws, and hard hats. For furnishing, featured recommendations include a rope swing, a bookshelf, a backpack of snacks, sleeping bags, and flashlights. Both the first page and the last suggest a pair of intangible ingredients: time and looking up. Higgins uses each element of her poetic list as a jumping-off point to explore captivating natural imagery with metaphors such as, “one soldier/in an army of trees”, “you can make your own sky/with spotlights and shadows”, and “the wind keeps them/snug with a rustle/which is a hush you can feel/in your bones”.
