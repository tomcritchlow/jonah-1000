Allow me to present Emily & the Golden Acorn by @ianarchiebeck. In this irresistible tale of high adventure, a girl and her younger brother find that their beloved oak tree has transformed overnight into a pirate ship, their neighborhood and the land beyond now covered by a vast ocean. Beck’s lively ink-and-watercolor illustrations convey the wonder and drama of their perilous voyage to the edge of the world and back. Is their experience real or imaginary?
