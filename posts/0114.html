Gina Farina and the Prince of Mintz

by Nancy Patz

ISBN 9780152308155 · Find on Bookshop.org

If it’s a merry and lighthearted affair you seek, then Nancy Patz’s Gina Farina and the Prince of Mintz could be just the thing. The title characters are crafted to generate conflict: a haughty monarch who forces his subjects to live their lives by his example and a free-spirited baker who laughs at authority.
