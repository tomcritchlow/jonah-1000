First Tomato

by Rosemary Wells

ISBN 9780803711754 · Buy on Bookshop.org

Here is a look at First Tomato, one of three volumes in the Voyage to the Bunny Planet series by Rosemary Wells. Claire spends a disheartening day at school, confounded by both academics and physical education. While waiting for the bus during a snowstorm, she is spirited away to the Bunny Planet by the kindly Bunny Queen Janet, who shows her through a mystical arch and grants her the day she should have had. Claire finds herself outside a cottage in a quiet seaside village, where her mother asks her to pick a beautifully ripe tomato and other fresh vegetables for a delicious soup. In the bus on the way home, she dozes off, happy and relaxed. Wells grants her protagonist’s wish in fantasy rather than reality, employing a startling textual shift from third-person prose to inviting first-person anapestic rhyming couplets.
