The Fifth and Final Sun

by C. Shana Greger

ISBN 9780395674383 · Find on Bookshop.org

The book of the day is The Fifth and Final Sun, an Aztec creation myth adapted and illustrated by C. Shana Greger. In a primordial time, the earth is dim, lit only by the White Evening Star God until she bursts after a pointed insult from Tezcatlipoca, The God of the Night. After that, Tezcatlipoca himself becomes the first sun, followed by a succession of gods: Quetzalcoatl, God of the Wind, then Tlalloc, God of Rain, Thunder, and Lightning, then Tlalloc’s wife Chalchihuitlicue, Goddess of the Waters, and finally the humble Nanautzin, known as the Scabby-Pimply one.
