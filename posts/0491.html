Owl Lake

by Keizaburo Tejima

ISBN 9780399214264 · Buy on Bookshop.org

For a gently compelling glimpse of the majesty of nature, here is Owl Lake by Keizaburo Tejima, known mononymously as Tejima. A family of owls awakens at dusk. Father Owl swoops over the lake in search of food. He catches a large fish and brings it back to the nest to share with the others. Thus do the owls pass their time until dawn, when they will go to sleep.
