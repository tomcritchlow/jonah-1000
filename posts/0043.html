For today, we’ve got The Terrible Troll-Bird by Ingri and Edgar Parin d’Aulaire. The married author-illustrators gaily retell a Norwegian folktale whose eponymous fowl gives off a somewhat cheeky air of menace. The children of a quiet farm family must alert their mother to the danger, subdue the monstrous bird, and later contend with the looming threat of its wicked troll owners.
