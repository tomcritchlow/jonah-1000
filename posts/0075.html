Sky Tree

by Thomas Locker and Candace Christiansen · illustrated by Thomas Locker

ISBN 9780060248840 · Buy on Bookshop.org

If you’re in the mood for a dazzling blend of science and art, why not read Thomas Locker and Candace Christiansen’s Sky Tree? This book invites us to slow down and consider the everyday wonders of the progression of time in nature. Every page presents the exact same view of a single tree, but all at different times of day and night, during different seasons of the year.
