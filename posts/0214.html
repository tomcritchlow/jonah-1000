How to Paint the Portrait of a Bird

by Jacques Prévert · illustrated by Mordicai Gerstein

ISBN 9781596432154 · Find on Bookshop.org

What if you opened what appeared to be a nondescript informational manual and discovered a philosophical treatise suffused with artistic beauty? This unorthodox premise may well have inspired How to Paint the Portrait of a Bird. The text of the book, originally a 1945 poem by Jacques Prévert “Pour Faire le Portrait d’un Oiseau”, is here translated from the French and illustrated by Mordicai Gerstein. A series of instructions details the imaginative process of painting a bird, the act of capturing the bird’s image represented by magically capturing the bird itself on canvas. Gerstein renders the poem’s lush imagery with great sensitivity, creating a young painter protagonist to ground the emotional journey. The translation is largely faithful, with two notable departures. Prévert’s uncompromising passage, “Si l’oiseau ne chante pas/C’est mauvais signe/Signe que le tableau est mauvais” (“If the bird doesn’t sing, it’s a bad sign, a sign that the painting is bad”) is softened by Gerstein to “If it doesn’t sing, don’t be sad.
