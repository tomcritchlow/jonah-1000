Sometimes you just can’t sleep. Typically, the stress of a restless night stems from secondary concerns: how will this lack of rest affect me tomorrow? But perhaps the anxiety would melt away if you could embrace the state of wakefulness. And in The Insomniacs (written by @wolfandfox and illustrated by @benjaminhilts and @seanpeterhilts, also known as @brothershilts) a family does just that. A family moves twelve times zones and finds themselves unable to adjust to the jet lag. Their creative solution: nocturnality. Wolf plays her oddball premise seriously, allowing her characters to succeed by achieving a quirky new equilibrium in their lives.
