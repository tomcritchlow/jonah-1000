When the Moon Forgot

by Jimmy Liao

ISBN 9780316052689 · Buy on Bookshop.org

The book of the day is Jimmy Liao’s When the Moon Forgot. This touching story depicts an unexpected encounter and its cosmic impact on the world at large. The moon falls out of the sky and lands on Earth, where it is rescued by a young boy. A cottage industry of artificial moons springs up to provide people with light, but these replacements are soon discarded. Eventually, boy dons a wolf suit and ventures out into the world to try to help his friend return to the sky. Liao’s spare text (translated from Chinese by Sarah L. Thomson), interspersed with long wordless sequences, provides a contemplative atmosphere.
