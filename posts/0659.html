The Wild Swans

by Amy Ehrlich · illustrated by Susan Jeffers

ISBN 9780333492239 · Find on Bookshop.org

The Week of the Brothers Turned into Birds has woven its winged spell, and to conclude I present Hans Christian Andersen’s The Wild Swans, retold by Amy Ehrlich and illustrated by Susan Jeffers. Ehrlich’s eloquent writing honors the language of Andersen’s original text. Jeffers creates entrancing, detailed art by first laying down a pencil drawing, then applying fine-line pen, adding color with inks and dyes, and finally erasing the original pencil lines. She achieves shading and texture in her compositions through the use of shading, cross-hatching, and an intricate pebbled texture. A note for folklore enthusiasts: this tale was originally written by Danish writer Hans Christian Andersen and published in 1838 in Eventyr, fortalte for Børn. Ny Samling. Første Hefte (Fairy Tales Told for Children. New Collection. First Booklet).
