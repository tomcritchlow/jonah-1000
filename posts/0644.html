Here is a look at Gulliver’s Adventures in Lilliput, adapted by Ann Keay Beneduce from Jonathan Swift’s classic novel Gulliver’s Travels and illustrated by Gennady Spirin. Dr. Lemuel Gulliver sets sail for a voyage to the East Indies, but his ship is wrecked along the way and he finds himself at the mercy of a race of tiny humans in the strange land of Lilliput.
