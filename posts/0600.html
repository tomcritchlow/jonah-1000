Thomas Locker knows, as do we all, that water flows ceaselessly through our bodies and the world around us, nourishing and empowering every aspect of humanity and its environment. In his elegant book Water Dance, he creates a thrilling ode to that essential building block of life. Locker’s text takes the form of lyrical prose poetry, exploring the nature of water from a first-person perspective in landscapes and biomes around the world.
