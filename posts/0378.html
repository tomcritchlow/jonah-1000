The Prince and the Mermaid

by Ian Deuchar

ISBN 9780416115529 · Find on Bookshop.org

The book of the day is The Prince and the Mermaid, a magical tale by Ian Deuchar. After her clan shelters in a forest stream for the winter, a mermaid remains behind in the spring. When she sees a prince visiting the woods, she falls in love with him from afar and one day recites an incantation to transform herself into a human like him. When the young man brings his mysterious new bride to court, his childhood sweetheart grows bitter and vengeful; in her rage and despair, she employs the dark powers of a witch to sow doubt in the prince’s heart and sunder his happy relationship. With eloquent prose and appealing art, Deuchar spins a grand tale of joy, envy, and loss. Though the eponymous lovers are forever separated, the mermaid returns each month at the full moon to watch over her beautiful sleeping son.
