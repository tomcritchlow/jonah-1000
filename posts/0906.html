Rise the Moon

by Eileen Spinelli · illustrated by Raúl Colón

ISBN 9780803726017 · Find on Bookshop.org

Eileen Spinelli and @raul.colon.7140 present a soothing hymn to the night in Rise the Moon. The world sleeps. In the glowing darkness, moths flutter, sea turtles crawl, wolves howl, a dancer performs a graceful movement, a baker prepares the crust for an apple pie, a sailor navigates over gentle waters, and a mother rocks her sleeping baby. Life moves this way and that, cradled by the moon’s gentle touch.
