The book of the day is William Steig’s Solomon the Rusty Nail. A young rabbit inexplicably gifted with the innate power to turn himself into a rusty nail at will is captured by a vicious cat, who imprisons him in a cage and later hammers him into a wall. Only by the happenstance of a timely house fire is he able to free himself and escape.
