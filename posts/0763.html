The Wicked Tricks of Tyl Uilenspiegel

by Jay Williams · illustrated by Friso Henstra

ISBN 9780590074780 · Find on Bookshop.org

Jay Williams and @frisohenstra invite you to a delightfully anarchic good time with The Wicked Tricks of Tyl Uilenspiegel. The book takes place in various cities across the Netherlands. Tyl Uilenspiegel, an incorrigible prankster, arouses the ire of the citizenry wherever he goes. He often makes restitution by turning his rapier-sharp wit on the invading Spanish forces, swindling the occupiers and delivering the ill-gotten gains to the Dutch folk. In the four stories contained in this volume, Tyl brings down a Spanish-held stronghold by walking a tightrope, incapacitates a Spanish governor with a ball of taffy, humiliates a Dutch plutocrat to satisfy a wager with Prince William of Orange, and feeds a hungry town at Christmas by stuffing an entire banquet into Spanish cannons.
