The Six Swans

by Anthea Bell · illustrated by Dorothée Duntze

ISBN 9780698205529 · Find on Bookshop.org

Another version of the Brothers Turned into Birds arrives in this adaptation of The Six Swans, translated by Anthea Bell and illustrated by Dorothée Duntze. Duntze’s alluring illustrations reveal a wealth of fascinating details, including the use of fox imagery on the witch’s daughter and the furtive reappearance of the witch herself in miniature form in the young king’s garden. A note about the text: Bell’s translation for this edition was later reillustrated in a version by Gerda Raidt, which was the first book I featured during this very theme week.
