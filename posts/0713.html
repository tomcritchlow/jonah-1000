For a tender look at the sensory experiences of youth, here is @nikkimcclure’s delicate and delightful In. A young child muses on the emotional attachments that keep us connected to our surroundings: First, a longing for the warmth, coziness, and familiarity of indoors. Next, a curiosity for the wildness, energy, and excitement of outdoors. Finally, a return to the indoors and a safe, relaxing bedtime.
