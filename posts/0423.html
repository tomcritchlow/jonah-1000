Sleeping Beauty

by Margaret Early

ISBN 9780810938359 · Find on Bookshop.org

Continuing with Sleeping Beauty Week, I’m looking through this appealing version by Margaret Early, whose dramatic text and illustrations (executed in oils on synthetic canvas and augmented with gold paint) create an air of antique refinement. The wicked fairy, with her sharp claws, snaggletooth, warty nose, and lazy eye, stands in grotesque contrast to her dainty sistren (though she shares their diaphanous wings). A note for folklore enthusiasts: Early has based her retelling on “La Belle au Bois Dormant” (“The Sleeping Beauty in the Wood”) from Charles Perrault’s Histoires ou Contes du Temps Passé, published in 1697. Notably, the number of fairies here is seven, with the wicked fairy making eight (in the Grimms’ variant, they are twelve with the wicked fairy making thirteen).
