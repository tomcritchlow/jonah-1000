The First Song Ever Sung

by Laura Krauss Melmed · illustrated by Ed Young

ISBN 9780140554571 · Buy on Bookshop.org

Take a heartfelt imagined voyage back to a primordial society in The First Song Ever Sung, written by Laura Krauss Melmed and illustrated by Ed Young. In this touching narrative, a young boy speaks separately to each member of his family—his father, his brother, his sister, his grandmother, his grandfather, his dog, the minnows in the brook, the birds in the sky, and finally his mother. He asks each to tell him about the first song ever sung, and the replies illuminate the speakers’ minds and hearts, as well as their unique relationships with the boy.
