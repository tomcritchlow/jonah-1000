The Twelve Dancing Princesses

by John Cech · illustrated by Lucy Corvino

Let us take a bow as the entrancing strains of Twelve Dancing Princesses Week draws to a close. For a coda, here is John Cech and Lucy Corvino‘s marvelous rendition of the tale. Cech adapts the text of the Brothers Grimm into a cheerful and lively retelling, capped off with an author’s note tracing the story’s motifs into the wider folklore canon.
