Galileo's Treasure Box

by Catherine Brighton

ISBN 9780802787682 · Buy on Bookshop.org

For a gentle portrait of a character from the margins of history, try Galileo’s Treasure Box by Catherine Brighton. Virginia, daughter of Galileo Galilei, lives a happy life in seventeenth-century Italy. When she sees a mysterious box in her father’s study, she cannot resist opening it. Inside are two clear lenses, two pieces of colored glass, and a feather. Though she does not understand the items’ significance, examining their physical properties inspires her and brings her closer to her father.
