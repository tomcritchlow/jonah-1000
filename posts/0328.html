Perfect

by Max Amato

ISBN 9780545829311 · Find on Bookshop.org

Sometimes a premise is fueled by the conflict between two characters so diametrically opposed that you can’t believe no one hit upon it sooner. Perfect by @max_amato_ gives us an eraser whose maintenance of a pristine white sheet of paper is thwarted by the antics of a mischievous pencil. Not content with simply taunting the eraser, the pencil soon gains the upper hand by turning the backdrop itself into a forest of graphite. However, the eraser learns to embrace imperfection, creating lines and images of its own by clearing paths of white through the gray, and a lasting peace is achieved.
