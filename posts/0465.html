Tenzin’s Deer

by Barbara Soros · illustrated by Danuta Mayer

ISBN 9781841488110 · Find on Bookshop.org

For a heartwarming and enlightening experience, read Tenzin’s Deer by Barbara Soros and Danuta Mayer. In old Tibet, Tenzin, a boy revered for his kindness and preternatural wisdom finds a musk deer wounded by a hunter’s arrow. He names her Jampa and gradually nurses her back to health, guided by knowledge from his dreams. Boy and deer become fast friends, but eventually it is time for the animal to return to the wild. The two part ways, but Jampa returns to Tenzin in his dreams, granting peace and spiritual balance.
