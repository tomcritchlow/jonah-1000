The Sea Tiger

by Victoria Turnbull

ISBN 9781783700073 · Find on Bookshop.org

It’s an imaginary journey beneath the waves you’ll discover when you open The Sea Tiger by @vic_turnbull. The eponymous character, a jovial and nurturing tiger, narrates the story, detailing his adventures with a young merboy named Oscar. The two travel the seas, witnessing myriad ocean marvels and miracles, perfectly happy together. The Sea Tiger both entertains and protects the merboy, acting as a hybrid of playmate and surrogate parent. Nevertheless, Oscar must eventually learn to make new friends—and the opportunity arises when they meet another merchild who also swims alongside a magical undersea companion: a Lion.
