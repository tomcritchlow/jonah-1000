The Princess’s Blankets

by Carol Ann Duffy · illustrated by Catherine Hyde

ISBN 9780763645472 · Find on Bookshop.org

There’s a remarkable synesthesia at work on the pages of Carol Ann Duffy and @catherinehydeartist’s The Princess’s Blankets. Duffy gives us the pensive tale of a princess incapable of feeling warmth and an ominous stranger whose perverse attempts to present her with a suitable blanket threaten the very order and structure of the natural world.
