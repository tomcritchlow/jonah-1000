East o’ the Sun and West o’ the Moon

illustrated by P.J. Lynch

ISBN 9780744519273 · Find on Bookshop.org

Today’s book is East o’ the Sun and West o’ the Moon, with text adapted from George Webbe Dasent’s translation in Popular Tales from the Norse and illustrations by the ingenious @pjlynchgallery. In many of his images, particularly the two-page spreads, Lynch uses extreme angles and stark lighting contrast to create breathtakingly dramatic composition. Through their outrageous poses and facial expressions, his characters come vividly to life.
