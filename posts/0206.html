Cinderella

by Ruth Sanderson

ISBN 9780316779654 · Buy on Bookshop.org

Second in my weeklong project of featuring a new version of Cinderella each day, we have a lovely edition adapted and illustrated by @ruthsandersonart. This iteration foregrounds Cinderella’s loneliness and despair at the start of the plot, giving way to a pleasingly magic-tinged conclusion. Both the elegant text and the stately oil paintings contribute to an air of refinement and grandeur throughout the book. A note for folklore enthusiasts: Sanderson bases her retelling on Charles Perrault’s 1697 narrative from Histoires ou Contes du Temps Passé. To this foundation she adds a lovely tree and a helpful bird, elements from the Brothers Grimm’s 1812 variant of the tale from Kinder- und Hausmärchen (with some notably macabre scenes of mutilation judiciously excised).
