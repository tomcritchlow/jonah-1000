Paradise Sands

by Levi Pinfold

ISBN 9781529520040 · Find on Bookshop.org

Consider, Gentle Readers, that with the passage of time we may find magic and danger echoing from generation to generation. So it goes in @levi_pinfold’s eerie, poetic tale Paradise Sands. The story opens with a young girl and her three brothers driving through the desert to meet their mother. They stop to pick flowers and find their way to a palatial hotel with no one inside. Tugged onward by the string of destiny and an eldritch nursery rhyme, the boys drink water from a pool, eat food from a table, and are soon ensnared by an unearthly power. The Teller, the edifice’s mysterious leonine master, will only release them if their sister can sit at a banquet table for three days without consuming anything. The sister fulfills her end of the bargain, but because she has used a bit of water on some drooping flowers the Teller lays down a curse: her own children will one day find their way to him and repeat the ordeal. Later, the girl realizes that her mother too visited the Teller as a child. How far back in time does this cycle stretch? How long will it continue? What is the nature of the hotel and its sinister magic?
