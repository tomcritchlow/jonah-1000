Rumpelstiltskin

by Edith Tarcov · illustrated by Edward Gorey

ISBN 9780590073936 · Find on Bookshop.org

Another Rumpelstiltskin for your inspection, this one by Edith Tarcov and Edward Gorey. Tarcov closely adapts the text of the Brothers Grimm, though having fun with the queen’s first night of guessing by constructing a list of names in alphabetical order. The illustrations, in black and white with accents of yellow, provide the tale with Gorey’s characteristic air of eerie detachment.
