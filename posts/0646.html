Picturescape

by Elisa Gutiérrez

ISBN 9781894965651 · Find on Bookshop.org

For a silent yet eye-popping journey, have a look at Picturescape by Elisa Gutiérrez. On a field trip to an art museum, a boy is mysteriously transported inside the world of the art in the exhibits. He travels from painting to painting, seeing all there is to see, until his teacher is able to pull him out and return him safely to the world he knows.
