More, More, More, Said the Baby

by Vera B. Williams

ISBN 9780688147365 · Buy on Bookshop.org

On Thanksgiving, my wish for all is a warm house, a good meal, and the loving presence of family and friends. I am filled with the spirit of tenderness and gratitude whenever I read More, More, More, Said the Baby, a sweet ode to the bond between children and their caregivers by Vera. B Williams. The book is written as a collection of three toddler-centered love stories, featuring Little Guy and his Daddy, Little Pumpkin and his Grandma, and Little Bird and her Mama. Through soothing text and bright gouache art, Williams creates a charming celebration of childhood and family.
