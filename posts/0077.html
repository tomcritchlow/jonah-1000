Here’s a little something for your enjoyment: The Blue Jackal by Marcia Brown. In this engaging tale, adapted from the Panchatantra, an ancient Indian collection of fables believed to have been written originally in Sanskrit, a clever jackal known as Fierce-Howl takes advantage of an accidental tumble into a vat of indigo dye by passing himself off as a divine creature to a credulous pack of forest animals, who venerate him and crown him king.
