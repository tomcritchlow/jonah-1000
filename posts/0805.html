Mem Fox and Vivienne Goodman’s Guess What? brings a zany energy to a spare, irreverent character piece. The focal character is a so-called crazy lady named Daisy O’Grady. She is tall, thin, and fond of animals. She wears a black dress and hat, owns a black cat, and likes to fly at night. She is in fact a witch, but unlike her wicked sistren she is a harmless and charismatic figure.
