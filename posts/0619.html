Unplugged

by Steve Antony

ISBN 9781338187373 · Buy on Bookshop.org

We’ve all felt the discomfort of surrounding ourselves with technology and severing our relationship with nature. But what might be the reaction of a purely technological being upon experiencing nature for the very first time? This is the question asked—and satisfyingly answered—in Unplugged by @mrsteveantony. A power cable connects a small robot named Blip to a large computer, which keeps her constantly informed and entertained. One day, during a blackout, she stumbles, trips over the cord, bounces down the stairs, and rolls outside. Having never left the building before, Blip is delighted with her new surroundings. She frolics through the grass and trees with a fawn, a rabbit, and a duckling before returning home to plug in once more. However, she is now sustained by the joyous memory of her adventure.
