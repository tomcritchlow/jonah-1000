Ocean Meets Sky

by Eric Fan and Terry Fan

ISBN 9781786033871 · Buy on Bookshop.org

This poignant story of memories and emotions shot through with glimmering fantasy will surely bring a smile to your face. Let @ericfanillustration and @terryfanillustration (together known as @thefanbrothers) whisk you away in their marvelous work Ocean Meets Sky. A young boy named Finn lives by the sea. After the loss of his beloved grandfather, he builds a boat and sails off into a magical ocean adventure. His voyage takes him past the Library Islands, across a series of giant sea shells, and over a group of luminous moon jellies, until he reaches a place that Grandpa always described in his stories, a place where ocean meets sky.
