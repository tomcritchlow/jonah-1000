Once Upon a Twice

by Denise Doyen · illustrated by Barry Moser

ISBN 9780606321914 · Find on Bookshop.org

If you’re hungry for a darkly playful poetic lark, look no further than Once Upon a Twice, a dramatically zany story written by Denise Doyen and illustrated by Barry Moser. While traveling warily through the night, a group of mice tries to impress upon one of their number the importance of caution. The careless mouse, Jam, ignores his elders’ warnings and remains out in the open, enjoying the nocturnal environment. After nearly being devoured by a snake, however, he develops a lifelong understanding of and respect for the threats of nature.
