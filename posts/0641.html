Leaf

by Sandra Dieckmann

ISBN 9781911171317 · Find on Bookshop.org

The book of the day is Leaf by @sandradieckmann. All the animals are greatly disturbed when an enormous polar bear arrives in the forest and takes up residence in a cave. The creature soon gains the nickname Leaf, because of his odd habit of wrapping himself in leaves and jumping off a high ledge into a lake as if hoping to fly. Finally, the birds take the critical step of talking to the bear and discover that all of his wild, off-putting behavior is an attempt to escape the area, where he landed unintentionally on a piece of drifting ice. Once they understand his motives, the animals resolve to help Leaf return to his family.
