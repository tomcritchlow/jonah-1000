Earth Mother

by Ellen Jackson · illustrated by Leo and Diane Dillon

ISBN 9780802789921 · Find on Bookshop.org

Here is Earth Mother, an original tale written in lyrical prose by Ellen Jackson and illustrated with the gorgeous art of husband-and-wife team Leo and Diane Dillon. Earth Mother is depicted as the prime mover of creation, traveling at an unhurried pace throughout the land and administering to the material elements of life. She listens without judgment to the beings under her care, offering nourishment and renewal.
