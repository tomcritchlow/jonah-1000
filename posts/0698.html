Merlin's Castle

by László Gál

ISBN 9780773728523 · Find on Bookshop.org

If you’re in the mood for a strange and wonderful journey, check out Merlin’s Castle by László Gál. Raphaella (nicknamed Ralphy) and her brother Marco, along with Marco’s pet lizard Donatello, are transported inside a book designed by their father. There, the wizard Merlin guides them to a flying ship, which carries them through time. In the Middle Ages, Donatello grows to an enormous size and defeats an evil dragon. Finally, Merlin reappears and transports them home again.
