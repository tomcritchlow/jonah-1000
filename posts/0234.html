There’s a timeless quality to the mischievous encounter at the heart of The Fox and the Crow by Manasi Subramaniam and Culpeo S. Fox. In this adaptation of the well-known Aesop fable, a fox encourages a crow to sing and this tricks it into dropping a morsel of food from its mouth. Subramaniam conveys the plot with a lyrical author’s voice, crafting a prose poem in short, elliptical.
