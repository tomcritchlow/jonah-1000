My Life with the Wave

by Catherine Cowan · illustrated by Mark Buehner

ISBN 9780060562007 · Buy on Bookshop.org

What if your life suddenly got upended by the intrusion of a supernatural presence? Moreover, what if, instead of remaining with you only for a brief period of time, the otherworldly entity simply stayed and stayed and stayed until it became your new normal? You’ll ponder just such a predicament in My Life with the Wave, translated and adapted by Catherine Cowan (from a short story originally written in Spanish by Octavio Paz) and illustrated by Mark Buehner. A young boy playing at the seashore befriends a sentient wave, who follows him home and takes up residence with his family. While at first this bizarre scenario appears to bring nothing but delight, the capricious swell of water soon brings on dangerous weather and dredges up horrors from the deep. Buehner’s oil and acrylic paintings vividly capture the reflective surfaces and fluid dynamics of the swirling, frothing wave.
