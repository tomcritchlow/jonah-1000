Here is the first of the year… If aliens were arriving and planning to explore our world, what would they need to know? @sophieblackall begins with this powerful guiding question in her sweeping and thought-provoking work If You Come to Earth. The text serves as a primer for extraterrestrial visitors to our planet. A boy in a bright red Phrygian cap introduces facets of natural science and human culture all around the globe. Bodies, emotions, clothing, transportation, animals, plants, and manufactured objects are all examined with simple clarity and warmth as part of our lived experience. Inspired by her travels to Rwanda, India, Bhutan, the Democratic Republic of the Congo, and other countries, Blackall marries thoughtful text with delicate ink-and-watercolor art to create a simultaneously epic and intimate whole.
