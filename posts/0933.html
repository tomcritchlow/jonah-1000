Creepy Castle

by John S. Goodall

ISBN 9780689822056 · Find on Bookshop.org

Let’s take a quick trip through a lighthearted wordless adventure, shall we? John S. Goodall presents a tour of the unknown and macabre in the delightful Creepy Castle. Two mice, a warrior and a princess, explore a large, abandoned manor, where they encounter dusty corridors, hanging bats, and a menacing dragon. Unbeknownst to them, they are stalked the whole way by a villainous mouse who bears them ill will. Finally, after jumping out a window into the moat and rowing to shore with a helpful frog, they take their pursuer by surprise and knock him into the water. Then, they flee to the safety of home.
