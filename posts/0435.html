The Rose and the Tree Frog

by Warja Lavater

ISBN 9783858851048 · Buy on Bookshop.org

For a fascinating exercise in postmodern storytelling, see what you make of The Rose and the Tree Frog by Warja Lavater. The art, though unaccompanied by text, conveys a definite narrative. Visual elements on each page operate not only as illustrations but as encoded symbols. A key at the beginning of the book relates each figure to a passage of text, such that the story can be followed through the abstract action of each page.
