Little Parsley

by Enzo Giannini

ISBN 9780671671976 · Find on Bookshop.org

Here is an enchanting story shot through with dark magic: Little Parsley, retold and illustrated by Enzo Giannini. When a pregnant woman cannot resist tasting parsley from the garden next door, the witches who own it demand ownership over her unborn daughter. Little Parsley, as she is named, is taken by the old hags several years later, forced to live in their house and obey their orders. With each seemingly impossible task, the witches’ cousin Nino agrees to help Little Parsley, and the two are eventual able to vanquish the wicked crones once and for all. Giannini’s text and art brighten the mood of the otherworldly proceedings. A note for folklore enthusiasts: Giannini has adapted this story from “La Prezzemolina” (Little Parsley), a folktale collected by Vittorio Imbriani in La Novellaja Fiorentina (1877). The plot combines elements of multiple tale types.
