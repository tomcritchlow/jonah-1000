Pigs from A to Z

by Arthur Geisert

ISBN 9780395385098 · Buy on Bookshop.org

Come find your way through the series of letter-themed tableaux in Arthur Geisert’s Pigs from A to Z. Part concept book, part original story, the narrative follows the construction of a colossal treehouse. The plucky group of pigs employs a succession of mechanical devices and steam-powered machines in order to accomplish their extraordinary feats of engineering.
