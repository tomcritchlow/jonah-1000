There’s an uncommonly tactile energy pervading the visual world of Stephen Gammell’s lighthearted fantasy adventure story, Twigboy. The plot opens in a dark and sinister Weedland. Twigboy, a sentient, ambulatory stick is trapped by a gang of hostile Snackerpinchers. Luckily, an animate rock comes rolling uncontrollably down from above, scattering the wicked bugs before plunging headlong into a swamp. Twigboy pulls him to safety, names him Rockwell, and brings him home to meet Granma and Granpa Twig. Later, the new friends hatch a plot for revenge, and jump willingly back into Weedland, the story ending on a raucous cliffhanger.
