Aladdin and the Magic Lamp

illustrated by Greg Hildebrandt

ISBN 9780590464178 · Find on Bookshop.org

The magic in the heart of Aladdin Week spills forth with Aladdin and the Magic Lamp, an adaptation illustrated by Greg Hildebrandt. The cheerful text and bright colors make for a light and entertaining read. Fascinatingly, the text avoids mentioning the death of the wicked magician by having the genie suddenly drag him into the lamp—an unexpectedly dramatic intercession. An observation: the designs of several characters and locations are strongly reminiscent of a well-known animated film adaptation of Aladdin…released in the same year as this book.
