Journey

by Aaron Becker

ISBN 9781406355345 · Find on Bookshop.org

Today I am delighted to present the first chapter in a trilogy that I consider the premiere wordless picture book achievement of the past decade: @storybreathing’s Journey. Unable to hook her family’s attention, a girl sits at home, bored and alone. Suddenly she spots something bright red. Is it a crayon? A marker? A piece of chalk? No matter. She picks it up, makes a door on her bedroom wall, passes through, and suddenly finds herself in an alternate universe, a thrilling fantasy world of high action and adventure. She travels across the mysterious landscape from an ethereal forest hung with glowing lanterns to a grand castle crisscrossed by locks and canals to a massive and imposing airship above the clouds to a city of domes and minarets to a desert under a starry sky. All the while, she uses her drawing implement to create handy vehicles—a rowboat, a hot air balloon, a flying carpet. Finally, she returns to her own familiar world, only to discover that she may not be as alone as she thinks.
