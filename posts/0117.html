Johnny Longnose

by James Krüss · illustrated by Stasys Eidrigevičius

ISBN 9781558580237 · Find on Bookshop.org

We all have things about us that make us stand out, and in this way perhaps we are all a bit like Johnny Longnose. Originally published in Switzerland under the title Hans Naselang, this book features surreal paintings by Stasys Eidrigevičius and German poems by James Krüss (translated into English by Naomi Lewis).
