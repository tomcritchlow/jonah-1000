Imagine a Night

by Sarah L. Thomson · illustrated by Rob Gonsalves

ISBN 9781442440685 · Buy on Bookshop.org

Here is Imagine a Night, a wondrous fusion of the writing of Sarah L. Thomson and the art of Rob Gonsalves. This collection of words and pictures outlines a marvelous night where anything can happen. Lights flicker out of the darkness, haunting figures come to life, and strange events occur without warning. Thomson’s poetic phrases (“a quilt of fields and forests”, “a lullaby for leaf and stem/and dreaming root”, “candlelight rises/on butterfly wings”) evoke startling and thrilling fantasy images. In his ethereal acrylic paintings, Gonsalves depicts uncanny shifts in reality through dreamlike transformations and reflections.
