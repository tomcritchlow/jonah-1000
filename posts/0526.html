Out of the Nursery, Into the Night

by Kathleen Hague · illustrated by Michael Hague

ISBN 9780517169704 · Find on Bookshop.org

When you were a child, what marvelous things did you believe waited beyond the threshold of sleep? Married author-illustrator team Kathleen and Michael Hague present their charming vision in Out of the Nursery, Into the Night. A sequence of teddy bears visits magical worlds to take on wondrous new identities: a cowgirl, a knight, a circus clown, a mermaid, a wizard.
