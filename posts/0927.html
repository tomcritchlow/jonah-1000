The Firebird

by Bernard Lodge

ISBN 9780613352055 · Buy on Bookshop.org

Firebird Sequel Week continues with a cheerful version by Bernard Lodge. When a tsar’s apples are mysteriously stolen, his three sons try to catch the culprit in the act. While the two older princes fall asleep on the job, young Ivan discovers that a Firebird is robbing his father’s tree. Thus begins a quest in which Ivan must obtain a magic horse to trade for the Firebird and a fair princess to trade for the horse, all with the help of a clever, magical gray wolf. Lodge’s bright colors and merry line art make for a winning adaptation.
