A Dark Dark Tale

by Ruth Brown

ISBN 9780862640019 · Find on Bookshop.org

Next up in my pre-Halloween scary book week: Ruth Brown’s A Dark Dark Tale, based on a traditional children’s chant of obscure origin. With a striking absence of characters (save a lone black cat), a sequence of eerie locations and landmarks unfolds from page to page, beckoning the reader onward from a dark, dark moor into a dark, dark house, where up a dark, dark stair lies a dark, dark room...Brown creates an atmosphere of ominous and sepulchral beauty with paintings that evoke the mystery and elegance of Gothic horror.
