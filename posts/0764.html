Brother Sun, Sister Moon

by Katherine Paterson · illustrated by Pamela Dalton

ISBN 9781452105796 · Buy on Bookshop.org

Revel in the quiet beauty of Katherine Paterson and Pamela Dalton’s Brother Sun, Sister Moon. Adapted from Saint Francis of Assisi’s Canticle of the Creatures, this paean to the sacred simplicity of life forms a transcendent understanding of God through the simple glory and wonder of existence. Paterson’s compelling text radiates spiritual energy, reverent verses celebrating the sun, the moon, wind, water, fire, and death.
