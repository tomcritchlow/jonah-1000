My Friend the Starfinder

by George Ella Lyon · illustrated by Stephen Gammell

ISBN 9781416927389 · Buy on Bookshop.org

George Ella Lyon and Stephen Gammell combine their talents to produce a work of otherworldly beauty: My Friend the Starfinder. A woman relates the story of a long-ago acquaintance, an old man she knew when she was a young girl. She passes along stories he shared with her about his own youth, wondrous tall tales of chasing down fallen stars and melding with the colors of the rainbow.
