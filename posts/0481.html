The Rainbow Serpent

by Dick Roughsey

ISBN 9780529055583 · Find on Bookshop.org

Here is Dick Roughsey’s The Rainbow Serpent, a compelling etiological myth. The story opens in the primordial world of Dreamtime, a bare expanse where people dwell without plants or animals. Goorialla, the great Rainbow Serpent, travels here and there, the movements of his body creating mountains, gorges, rivers, and lagoons. He introduces his tribe of humans to sacred dances and ceremonial attire. However, when the giant snake impulsively swallows two boys looking for shelter from the rain, he flees to evade the retribution of the community. The people’s rescue effort and the ensuing conflict reshape the land even further, leading to the order of our modern world.
