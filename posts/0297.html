Ignis

by Gina Wilson · illustrated by P.J. Lynch

ISBN 9782700042757 · Find on Bookshop.org

It’s difficult (and perhaps all too common) to feel like a failure surrounded by success. For some of us, it’s merely our perception, a case of so-called “Impostor Syndrome”. For the dragon protagonist of Gina Wilson and @pjlynchgallery’s Ignis, however, the evidence is plain to see; unlike all the others in his community, he cannot breathe fire. How, he wonders, can he be a real dragon without fire? If he is in fact destined for a flame all his own, where is it? Ignis sets off on a journey to discover his true nature and awaken the spark within. Wilson fleshes out her engaging premise with a cheerful atmosphere and pleasingly tactile details.
