The Children of Lir

by Sheila MacGill-Callahan · illustrated by Gennady Spirin

ISBN 9780803711228 · Find on Bookshop.org

Sheila MacGill-Callahan and Gennady Spirin present a lyrical Irish tale in The Children of Lir. A king with twin sons and twin daughters marries his late wife’s sister. The new queen, long having nursed a grudge against her nieces and nephews, turns them into four swans. Having lost his beloved family, the king goes mad with grief, and the queen takes over the kingdom. With the help of hordes of animals, including an enormous talking whale, the four siblings must break the queen’s evil curse and return to human form.
