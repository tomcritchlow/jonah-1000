Rapunzel

by Brothers Grimm · illustrated by Maja Dusíková

ISBN 9780613267151 · Buy on Bookshop.org

The fairy tale theme week is back, and this time I’ll be delving into seven different versions of Rapunzel. We begin with a version translated from the German of the Brothers Grimm by Anthea Bell and illustrated by @majadusikova. Soft washes of color create a lush and pleasant landscape; Rapunzel’s tower rises high from among the trees, resembling a half-timbered cottage with shingled roof. A note for folklore enthusiasts: Bell, working from the seventh edition of Kinder- und Hausmärchen, includes the detail of Rapunzel’s plan to weave a ladder out of silk so that she might escape from the tower. She translates “zauberin” as “witch” (instead of the more accurate “sorceress”).
