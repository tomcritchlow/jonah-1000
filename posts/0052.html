Reaching back into my childhood archive, I rediscovered David Gifaldi and C. Shana Greger’s The Boy Who Spoke Colors, an original tale of love, magic, greed, sorrow, and understanding. A child’s wondrous gift catches the attention of a cruel king, who separates him from his family in an attempt to harness the miracle for profit. Through examination of the characters and their motives, Gifaldi achieves a cautionary tale, moral but not overtly moralistic.
