The Glass Mountain

by Nonny Hogrogian

ISBN 9780517001035 · Find on Bookshop.org

Here’s an engrossing story of struggle, devotion, and enchantment: Nonny Hogrogian’s The Glass Mountain, adapted from the Brothers Grimm fairy tale “The Raven”. A young man encounters a princess transformed into a bird by her mother’s thoughtless curse, and must navigate a series of otherworldly obstacles to rescue her.
