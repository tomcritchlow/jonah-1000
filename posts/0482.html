The House in the Night

by Susan Marie Swanson · illustrated by Beth Krommes

ISBN 9781451770544 · Find on Bookshop.org

On Thanksgiving, I look around and remind myself just how lucky I am to have all the things that keep me nourished, safe, and comfortable. For a volume that captures a bit of that gratitude, here’s The House in the Night by Susan Marie Swanson and Beth Krommes. The poetic text pushes slowly and gently inward from a house to a bed to a book to illustrations of a light and darkness within. We then reverse course, pulling outward to find that same glowing light and sweet darkness in the world all around. Swanson creates a soothing text in the form of a “nesting rhyme”, inspired by “This Is the Key to the Kingdom” and other traditional nursery rhymes; Krommes provides black and white scratchboard art with spots of warm yellow watercolor. The book fills me with delight and relaxation, and I hope that feeling reaches out and envelops all of you and your loved ones.
