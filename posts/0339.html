Apple

by Nikki McClure

ISBN 9781419703782 · Buy on Bookshop.org

The book of the day is Apple by @nikkimcclure. A series of woodcuts follows an apple from its harvesting in an orchard to its inclusion in a child’s school lunch to its decomposition and rebirth as a seedling after winter’s end. McClure’s cut-paper art consists of black and white images, save for several key items—the apple, the backpack that transports it, and the mittens on the hands that once held it.
