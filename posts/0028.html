Now this is a work of art: The Moon’s Revenge, written by Joan Aiken and illustrated by Alan Lee. For many years I hazily remembered my school librarian reading us this book, but I recalled neither title nor author nor illustrator—only brief flashes of plot. When I finally found it again, I was overjoyed. Aiken weaves a spellbinding tale of spirits and monsters, of grandfather clocks and mysterious violins, of moonlit sea walls and whispers behind old, cracked doors.
