The Museum of Everything

by Lynne Rae Perkins

ISBN 9780062986306 · Find on Bookshop.org

Imagine yourself in a perfect place, where you can look at the world in just the way you want to. In her delicate and pensive The Museum of Everything, @lynneraeperkins explores just such a marvelous landscape. A young boy makes sense of life by considering things one at a time or in discrete sets, mentally viewing them in unique “museums” of the mind. He creates a Museum of Things I Wonder About, a Museum of Islands, a Museum of Bushes, a Museum of Hiding Places, a Museum of Shadows, and a Sky Museum. He even creates a Museum of Little Things, right on his very own windowsill.
