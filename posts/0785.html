Nancy Willard and @marygrandpre have brought a smile to my face with the delightful and playfully titled Sweep Dreams. A man brings home a broom that soon begins to move about on its own. They dance through the house each evening, sweeping together in the moonlight. After the broom is stolen by a mysterious stranger, the man is devastated, and he cannot rest until, with the help of a wise fortune-teller, he recover his precious companion. Now, however, the broom has gained the power to sweep rainbows and stars in the sky; the man waits patiently below, content to watch his friend work miracles high above him.
