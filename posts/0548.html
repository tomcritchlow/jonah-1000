The book of the day is The Miami Giant by Arthur Yorinks and Maurice Sendak. Giuseppe Giaweeni, attempting to chart a passage from Italy to China, finds himself in Florida where he encounters a lost tribe of dancing giants: the Mishbookers of Miami. He convinces one of the giants, Joe, to accompany him to Europe and appear on stage. The Parisian audience, however, erupts in a panic and attacks Joe, who flees and returns home to be with the other giants.
