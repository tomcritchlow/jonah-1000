There’s a current of gentle humor running through @paulabossio’s The Line. In this wordless adventure, a little girl discovers a line running across the ground. She picks it up and begins to play with it, shaking it like a rope, sliding down it, swinging from it like a tree branch. The line accordingly shapes itself into myriad forms: billowing bubbles, a playful monkey, a rapt audience. Finally, there arises at one end a large, horned monster, which threatens to swallow the girl, but a helpful bear emerges from the opposite end to chase the monster away.
