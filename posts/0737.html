Did you often go swimming when you were a child? Did you peer into the water and imagine all sorts of strange sights down below? In that case, read JiHyeon Lee’s wordless Pool and you just might feel a pang of familiarity. A young boy goes swimming and immediately encounters a raucous crowd. He cautiously dives into the pool and swims down beneath the tumult, toward the bottom where he encounters a girl. Together, they explore the depths and find all manner of exotic marine life: schools of tiny fish, a forest of coral, horned snakes slithering out of holes, sharp-toothed sea beasts, many-legged spiders, and even a furry white whale. The children finally emerge from the pool, bonded by their fantastical shared experience.
