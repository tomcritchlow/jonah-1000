Master of All Masters

illustrated by Marcia Sewall

ISBN 9780448214337 · Find on Bookshop.org

You’ll get a chuckle out of this adaptation of the English folktale Master of All Masters by Marcia Sewall. A young woman take a job as a servant for an eccentric gentleman who has a strange nickname for each item in his home. He wishes to be referred to as “Master of all Masters”, his bed is his “barnacle”, his pants are “squibs and crackers”, his cat is “white-faced simminy”, the fire is “hot cockalorum”, the water is “pondalorum”, and the house itself is “high topper mountain”. At night, when a fire breaks out, the servant finds that she must construct a sentence containing every single one of these ridiculous monikers—an event that effectively serves as the punchline to the entire bizarre premise.
