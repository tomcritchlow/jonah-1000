How enigmatic, how thrillingly hallucinatory is The Story of Bip, a magic-realism-tinged biographical work written and illustrated by world-renowned mime Marcel Marceau. In a first person narrative, the author inhabits his real-life stage persona Bip as he is whisked away on an odyssey of self-discovery through outer space, to the moon, and back to Earth to share his discovery and experiences through performance in front of a vast, adoring crowd.
