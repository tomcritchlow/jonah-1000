The Hedgehog Boy

by Jane Langton · illustrated by Ilse Plume

ISBN 9780060236960 · Find on Bookshop.org

There’s a delightful mixture of humor and heart in The Hedgehog Boy, a Latvian folktale retold by Jane Langton and illustrated by Ilse Plume. A farmer and his wife dearly wish for a child. The mysterious and benevolent Forest Mother gifts them with a basket, which they later discover contains a baby boy covered in prickles like a hedgehog. Known as “the hedgehog boy”, this lad grows up to marry the king’s youngest daughter, along the way revealing a number of hidden talents, including the tending of pigs and the wrangling of roosters. Langton’s engaging prose and Plume’s illustrations (evocative of Latvian folk art) create a merry and winning blend.
