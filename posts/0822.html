Halloween has come again: the night when all things wild and fantastic become tantalizingly real. As we wander over darkened paths, jack-o’-lanterns grin eerily out of the gloom. For this night of fearsome nights I have for you Adrienne Adams’ A Halloween Happening. In this spiritual sequel to A Woggle of Witches, the merry coven decides to invite children to their annual celebration of All Hallows’ Eve. They construct an enormous tower of pumpkins around a leafless tree and welcome in a host of costumed children. After a long climb to the top, witches and children soar together through the sky on majestic bat-winged gliders.
