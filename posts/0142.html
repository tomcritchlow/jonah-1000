Never Satisfied

by Fulvio Testa

ISBN 9783855390120 · Find on Bookshop.org

How do we see our surroundings? Do we become so inured to our environment that we stop noticing what makes it special? This phenomenon of perception fuels the quirky Never Satisfied by Fulvio Testa. Two children stroll through their neighborhood, bemoaning the utter lack of excitement. Each complaint is belied by one of the many bizarre sights they pass, from furniture raining out a window to a woman tightrope-walking on a clothesline to a hail of arrows flying at a bullseye target.
