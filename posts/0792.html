The book of the day is Heartbeat by @evanturkart. A huge whale and its young offspring swim through the ocean, joining their whale song in aquatic harmony. One day, their happiness is violently sundered by a whale hunt. The baby whale is forced to swim through the oceans alone until it finds solace and connection in the voice of a young girl, whose voice acts as a balm and a guide.
