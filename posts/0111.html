A Visit to William Blake’s Inn

by Nancy Willard · illustrated by Alice and Martin Provensen

ISBN 9780676303681 · Find on Bookshop.org

Let’s go on a journey together, an exploration of poetry, fantasy, and imagination. All this and more can be found in the pages of A Visit to William Blake’s Inn, written by Nancy Willard and illustrated by Alice and Martin Provensen. Willard takes inspiration from Blake’s work to craft a rollicking collection of verse, chock-full of wonder and high-nonsense, wending its way through the marvelous goings-on at an imaginary inn run by the famed poet himself.
