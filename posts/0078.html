Many Moons

by James Thurber · illustrated by Louis Slobodkin

ISBN 9780152518738 · Find on Bookshop.org

Today I’m revisiting an old classic: James Thurber and Louis Slobodkin’s Many Moons. Young Princess Lenore, sick in bed, is sure she’ll be well again if her father can get her the moon. The king consults his Lord High Chamberlain, his Royal Wizard, and his Royal Mathematician, but only the Court Jester, armed with unexpectedly clever logic and respect for the power of imagination, can grant the girl’s request.
