The Squirrel Wife

by Philippa Pearce · illustrated by Wayne Anderson

ISBN 9781406300512 · Find on Bookshop.org

The book of the day is Philippa Pearce and Wayne Anderson’s The Squirrel Wife. Jack lives a dismal existence tending pigs under the thumb of his cruel older brother. One night, a storm fells a great beech tree in the forest, trapping one of the mysterious “green people”—a race of mysterious wood-dwelling sprites feared by humans. After freeing the little man and bringing him back to his clan, Jack is rewarded with a magic ring that allows him to transform a squirrel into a human, a young woman who becomes his wife. The couple makes a happy life together until Jack’s brother wrongfully accuses him of a crime and has him imprisoned. Only Jack’s squirrel wife can release him—but at what cost?
