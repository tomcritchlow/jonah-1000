Snow-White and the Seven Dwarfs

by Randall Jarrell · illustrated by Nancy Ekholm Burkert

ISBN 9780374370992 · Find on Bookshop.org

A staple of my childhood: Snow-White and the Seven Dwarfs, by poet Randall Jarrell and illustrator Nancy Ekholm Burkert. Jarrell’s translation remains scrupulously faithful to the German of the Brothers Grimm, while Burkert’s detailed paintings give the story in a series of arresting tableaux.
